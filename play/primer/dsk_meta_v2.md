# Duskmourn (DSK) Limited primer (v2)

Sources, in our own words: Draftsim DSK draft guide; Card Kingdom DSK draft guide; Marshall Sutcliffe (magic.gg);
Seth Manfield (Ultimate Guard); 17lands public game data, used only to choose which cards are listed.

## Format
- Medium speed; removal, two-for-ones and mana sinks (Rooms, impending, manifests) decide most games.
- Manifest dread: look at the top two, put one face down as a 2/2, the other into the graveyard. A face-down
  creature card turns up for its mana cost any time its owner has priority. If they milled a creature, the kept
  card is often better; a 2/2 attacking into bigger blockers with mana up may flip.
- Rooms: cast one door; unlock the other later at sorcery speed. Eerie: an enchantment enters under your control
  or you fully unlock a Room. Impending: an Overlord cast cheap is not a creature until its time counters are gone
  (one per its controller's end step), but its "enters or attacks" trigger happens on entry. Survival: second main
  phase, if it is tapped (it attacked). Delirium: four or more card types in your graveyard.
- In long games count both libraries: manifest, mill and surveil spend cards.

## Mandatory checks (every time; state the result in your reason)
- Before attacking: their untapped potential blockers (face-down 2/2s too) and which blocks kill your creature;
  their open mana and colors and the trick or flash card that would punish the attack ("Cards to respect");
  the crack-back: what they can swing with next turn and what you keep home.
- Before blocking: their possible trick (mana, colors); whether the trade favours you; life is a resource: take
  early hits to keep a creature that matters, chump only to survive. Check that not blocking doesn't leave you
  dead next turn to pump plus evasion.
- Before tapping out: which instant or flash card in their colors punishes it (removal on your only blocker,
  flash blocker, combat trick). If a sorcery-speed play can wait, play around the card that hurts most.
- Removal and tricks: keep hard removal for what beats you (fliers, engines, bombs; an indestructible Tyvar needs
  exile, -X/-X or an aura); don't spend it on a vanilla 2/2 without a tempo reason. Kill eerie/survival engines
  before they snowball. A trick wins a combat they chose or kills a blocker for lethal, not +2 damage.
- Mulligan (40 cards): keep 2-5 lands with plays by turn 3-4 (a 2-lander with cheap spells, on the draw).
  Mulligan 0-1 and 6-7 landers and hands missing a color. Keep a working 6; don't chase a better hand.

## Archetypes: plan, synergies, key cards, rares, how to beat it
- **WU eerie tempo.** Cheap enchantments and Rooms (Grand Entryway {1}{W} gives two eerie triggers, Fear of
  Surveillance {1}{W}, Meat Locker {2}{U}, Glimmerlight {2}) fuel Gremlin Tamer {W}{U} (1/1 per eerie), Optimistic
  Scavenger {W} (+1/+1 counter per eerie), Inquisitive Glimmer {W}{U} (discount). Wins with fliers and tokens while
  Unable to Scream {U}, Trapped in the Screen {2}{W}, Glimmerburst {3}{U} clear the way. Rares: Overlord of the
  Mistmoors, Entity Tracker, Ghostly Dancers, Dollmaker's Shop (tokens, then Porcelain Gallery). Against: kill the
  engine creature first; expect Floodpits Drowner and Jump Scare.
- **UB eerie control.** Trades, removes, draws, wins late in the air: Fear of Infinity {1}{U}{B} (flier, returns on
  eerie), Skullsnap Nuisance {U}{B}; Stay Hidden {1}{U}, Murder {1}{B}{B}, Winter's Intervention {1}{B}, Unnerving
  Grasp {2}{U}. Rares: Unholy Annex (Ritual Chamber's Demon turns its life loss into a drain), Kaito (ninjutsu),
  Overlord of the Floodpits. Against: pressure early; don't stake the game on one creature into Murder or counters.
- **BR sacrifice.** Fodder that leaves value (Innocuous Rat {1}{B}, Clockwork Percussionist {R}, Fear of Lost Teeth
  {B}, spent Rooms) feeds Final Vengeance {B}, Disturbing Mirth {B}{R} (draw 2), Sawblade Skinripper {1}{B}{R}
  (end-step damage), Popular Egotist {2}{B} (drain per sacrifice). Combos: Vengeful Possession {2}{R} steals a
  creature, then sacrifice it; Glassworks {2}{R} kills, then the Room is fodder. Rares: Unstoppable Slasher,
  Screaming Nemesis, Chainsaw. Against: trade rather than race; exile beats destroy; respect red burn.
- **RG delirium aggro.** Hasty threats (Wildfire Wickerfolk {R}{G}, Hand That Feeds {1}{R}) and fight removal
  (Beastie Beatdown {R}{G}, Monstrous Emergence {1}{G}, Coordinated Clobbering {G}). Say Its Name {1}{G}, Wary
  Watchdog {1}{G}, Spineseeker Centipede {2}{G}, used tricks, Rooms and artifacts reach delirium. Reach: Fear of
  Burning Alive, Violent Urge double strike. Rares: Valgavoth's Onslaught, Tyvar, Overlord of the Boilerbilges.
  Against: block early, keep life high; fights are sorcery speed, burn and pumps are not.
- **GW survival.** Attacks every turn: Shrewd Storyteller {1}{G}{W}, Glimmer Seeker {2}{W}, Defiant Survivor {2}{G};
  Baseball Bat {G}{W} taps a blocker, Hardened Escort {2}{W} makes an attacker indestructible, Horrid Vigor {1}{G}
  and Jump Scare {W} protect. Enduring Vitality (tap for mana) or Meat Locker on your own creature turns survival on
  without attacking. Rares: Rip, Kona, Unidentified Hovership. Against: keep blockers home so its attacks are bad.
- **WB reanimator.** Trades and removes (Murder, Trapped in the Screen, Seized from Slumber) while Say Its Name,
  Commune with Evil {2}{B}, Fanatic of the Harrowing {3}{B} fill the graveyard; then Emerge from the Cocoon {4}{W},
  Rite of the Moth {1}{W}{B}{B} or Valgavoth's Faithful {B} return Shroudstomper {3}{W}{W}{B}{B} (drain 2 and draw on
  entry and attack) or another big creature. Rares: Split Up (wipe the tapped creatures after they attack), Unholy
  Annex. Against: go under it; exile its best creature; Live or Die kills or reanimates at instant speed.
- **UR Rooms.** Rooms as removal and value (Glassworks {2}{R} 4 damage, Meat Locker {2}{U}, Ticket Booth {2}{R};
  Smoky Lounge {2}{R} ramps Rooms). Payoffs: Rampaging Soulrager {2}{R} (4/4 with two unlocked doors), Intruding
  Soulrager {U}{R} (sacrifice a spent Room: 2 damage, draw). Few creatures, long games. Rares: Roaring Furnace,
  Overlords. Against: attack while it sets up; doors unlock only at sorcery speed.
- **BG delirium midrange.** Self-mill (Say Its Name, Broodspinner {B}{G}, Commune with Evil) makes Drag to the
  Roots cost {B}{G} and grows Spineseeker Centipede and Osseous Sticktwister; Murder and big green bodies grind.
  Rares: The Swarmweaver, Valgavoth's Onslaught, Tyvar. Against: pressure it, it wins long; respect instant Murder
  and Drag to the Roots when you commit your best threat.
- **RW go-wide aggro.** The fastest deck. Creatures with power 2 or less: Arabella {R}{W} drains per small creature
  on attack, Midnight Mayhem {2}{R}{W} three hasty menace lifelinkers, Ragged Playmate {1}{R} unblockable,
  Vicious Clown {2}{R}, Most Valuable Slayer {3}{R} first strike on attack; tricks Turn Inside Out {R}, Jump Scare
  {W}, Violent Urge {R}. Rares: Screaming Nemesis, Toby, Overlord of the Mistmoors. Against: block early, trade, keep
  blockers; a small attacker into a bigger blocker with {R}/{W} open means a trick.
- **UG manifest midrange.** Manifest Dread {1}{G}, Growing Dread {G}{U} (flash; counter on each flip), Under the
  Skin {2}{G}, Unnerving Grasp {2}{U} give bodies and card choice; Cryptid Inspector {2}{G} grows on manifests and
  flips; Oblivious Bookworm {G}{U} loots without discarding after a manifest. Flip creatures mid-combat. Rares:
  Abhorrent Oculus, Valgavoth's Onslaught, Zimone. Against: a face-down 2/2 with mana open may flip.

## Cards to respect (instant speed: tricks, removal, flash). Check against their open mana and colors.
- White: Jump Scare {W} +2/+2 and flying; Seized from Slumber {1}{W} vs a tapped creature ({4}{W} otherwise),
  destroy; Unwanted Remake {W} destroy (they manifest); Shardmage's Rescue {W} flash aura, hexproof, +1/+1;
  The Wandering Rescuer (convoke, flash double strike).
- Blue: Floodpits Drowner {1}{U} flash 2/1, taps and stuns; Glimmerburst {3}{U} draw 2 + 1/1 blocker; Vanish
  from Sight {3}{U} tucks a permanent; counters: Don't Make a Sound {1}{U} (unless {2}), Twist Reality {1}{U}{U},
  Get Out {U}{U}, Fear of Impostors {1}{U}{U} (flash 3/2); flash Entity Tracker {2}{U}, Enduring Curiosity {2}{U}{U}.
- Black: Murder {1}{B}{B} destroy; Winter's Intervention {1}{B} 2 damage; Withering Torment {2}{B} destroy creature
  or enchantment; Nowhere to Run {1}{B} flash -3/-3; Give In to Violence {1}{B} +2/+2 lifelink; Appendage Amalgam
  {2}{B} flash 3/2; Live or Die {3}{B}{B} destroy or reanimate.
- Red: Scorching Dragonfire {1}{R} 3 damage; Betrayer's Bargain {1}{R} (+sacrifice or {2}) 5 damage; Impossible
  Inferno {4}{R} 6 damage; Turn Inside Out {R} +3/+0; Violent Urge {R} first strike (double with delirium);
  Untimely Malfunction {1}{R} two creatures can't block.
- Green: Horrid Vigor {1}{G} deathtouch + indestructible; Frantic Strength {2}{G} flash aura +2/+2 trample.
- Multicolor/colorless: Growing Dread {G}{U} flash manifest; Drag to the Roots {2}{B}{G} destroy nonland permanent;
  Bear Trap {1} flash, later {3}: 3 damage. Also face-down creatures flipping, Popular Egotist (indestructible),
  Tyvar (indestructible by tapping another creature).
