# Foundations (FDN) Limited primer (v2)

Sources, in our own words: Draftsim FDN draft guide; Card Kingdom FDN draft guide; Ultimate Guard FDN archetype
primer; 17lands public game data, used only to choose which cards are listed. Card text checked against XMage.

## Format
- A core set: evergreen keywords, few new rules. Games are medium to slow: cheap efficient removal means the early
  turns are mostly one-for-one trades, and card advantage, fliers and lifegain decide the long games. White is the
  deepest colour, green the weakest.
- Mechanics (ability words are reminders, not keywords; check the condition when it matters):
  Raid: a bonus if you attacked with a creature this turn (cast the Raid card after combat, in your second main
  phase). Morbid: a bonus if any creature died this turn (yours or theirs; tokens count). Threshold: a bonus while
  your graveyard has seven or more cards. Landfall: triggers each time a land enters under your control (play the land
  when the trigger helps most, e.g. before combat for Adventuring Gear). "Draw your second card each turn": the
  second card you draw in one turn, your draw step counts as the first (cantrips, loots, Think Twice on the
  opponent's turn turn it on). Flashback: cast it again from the graveyard for its flashback cost, then exile it.
  Kicker: an optional extra cost for a bigger effect. Prowess: +1/+1 until end of turn per noncreature spell you
  cast. Ward {N}: a spell or ability that targets it is countered unless its controller pays N. Surveil N: look at
  the top N, put any into the graveyard. Food ({2}, T, sacrifice: gain 3 life) and Treasure (T, sacrifice: one mana
  of any colour) are artifact tokens. Power 4 or greater: red-green cards check it.
- Lifegain payoffs (Ajani's Pridemate, Cat Collector, Marauding Blight-Priest, Fiendish Panda, Vanguard Seraph)
  trigger on every life gain event: lifelink damage, Food, Dazzling Angel, Sanguine Syphoner.
- In long games count both libraries: surveil, mill and looting spend cards; Threshold decks fill their own
  graveyard on purpose.

## Mandatory checks (every time; state the result in your reason)
- Before attacking: their untapped potential blockers and which blocks kill your creature (deathtouch, first strike
  during your turn on Inspiring Paladin and Quick-Draw Katana); their open mana and colours and the trick or flash
  card that would punish the attack ("Cards to respect"); the crack-back: what they can swing with next turn and
  what you keep home. Attacking also turns on your Raid cards this turn.
- Before blocking: their possible trick (mana, colours); whether the trade favours you; life is a resource: take
  early hits to keep a creature that matters, chump only to survive. Check that not blocking doesn't leave you
  dead next turn to pump plus evasion (fliers, Heroic Reinforcements, Overrun, Claws Out).
- Before tapping out: which instant or flash card in their colours punishes it (removal on your only blocker,
  flash blocker, counterspell, combat trick). If a sorcery-speed play can wait, play around the card that hurts most.
- Removal and tricks: keep hard removal for what beats you (fliers, engines, bombs, a lifegain payoff that is
  growing); don't spend it on a vanilla 2/2 without a tempo reason. A trick wins a combat they chose or kills a
  blocker for lethal, not +2 damage. Bite Down, Felling Blow and Bushwhack depend on your creature's power: count it.
  Hexproof (Snakeskin Veil, Celestial Armor) and ward ({2}) can fizzle your removal; hold it until they are tapped.
- Mulligan (40 cards): keep 2-5 lands with plays by turn 3-4 (a 2-lander with cheap spells, on the draw).
  Mulligan 0-1 and 6-7 landers and hands missing a colour. Keep a working 6; don't chase a better hand.

## Principles (from reviewed games)
- Count the race first: turns until you kill them and until they kill you (evasion, drains, pumps, lifegain). The one
  losing the race keeps blockers and trades; the one winning attacks. Plan only with cards you have, never with a
  hoped-for draw.
- Attack before casting creatures that don't change this combat; keep mana for the trick you hold. Raid cards go
  after combat.
- In a multi-block the attacker chooses which blocker dies: count it as losing your best blocker, so don't put an
  engine creature (a counter, card or token maker) in a block you can afford to skip. When you attack, the defender
  picks the block: count losing your best attacker to their best blocker.
- An Aura, Equipment or +1/+1 counter belongs on the creature that uses it (evasion, lifelink, first strike). A
  creature exposed to an edict, a sweeper or a tuck (Uncharted Voyage) is a bad host; ward and hexproof stop only
  targeted answers.
- Fetch or play the land whose colour unlocks the most cards in your hand.
- Don't tap out into a threat you must answer later; with removal in hand, spend it on what beats you.

## Archetypes: plan, synergies, key cards, rares, how to beat it
- **WU fliers.** The strongest pair. Cheap fliers (Healer's Hawk {W}, Dazzling Angel {2}{W}, Youthful Valkyrie
  {1}{W}, Vanguard Seraph {3}{W}, Strix Lookout {1}{U}, Spectral Sailor {U}, Mischievous Mystic {1}{U}) under Empyrean
  Eagle {1}{W}{U} (other fliers +1/+1), ground held by 3/4s and lifegain; tempo from Bigfin Bouncer {3}{U}, Run Away
  Together {1}{U}, Uncharted Voyage {3}{U}, Faebloom Trick {2}{U}. Rares: Kykar, Giada (Angels), Exemplar of Light,
  Curator of Destinies. Against: keep fliers or reach (Treetop Snarespinner) back, race on the ground; kill Eagle.
- **WB lifegain.** Small lifegain sources (Healer's Hawk, Dazzling Angel, Sanguine Syphoner {1}{B}, Cat Collector
  {2}{W}'s Food, Vampire Nighthawk {1}{B}{B}) feed Ajani's Pridemate {1}{W}, Marauding Blight-Priest {2}{B}, Fiendish
  Panda {2}{W}{B} (grows, returns a creature when it dies), Vanguard Seraph; Midnight Snack {2}{B} turns the gained
  life into damage. Grinds with Squad Rallier {3}{W}, Vampire Soulcaller {4}{B}, Reassembling Skeleton. Rares: Elenda,
  Bloodthirsty Conqueror, Exemplar of Light. Against: kill the payoff before it grows; exile beats destroy here.
- **UB threshold control.** Loots and surveils (Strix Lookout, Cephalid Inkmage {2}{U}, Lightshell Duo {3}{U},
  Icewind Elemental {4}{U}, Think Twice {1}{U}) fill the graveyard to seven for Dreadwing Scavenger {1}{U}{B} (flying,
  loots, deathtouch at threshold), Billowing Shriekmass {3}{B}, an unblockable Cephalid Inkmage; second-card payoffs
  (Mischievous Mystic, Erudite Wizard {2}{U}); removal Stab {B}, Bake into a Pie {2}{B}{B}, Eaten Alive {B}; counters
  Refute {1}{U}{U}, Essence Scatter {1}{U}. Rares: Kiora, Kaito, Liliana. Against: pressure early; play around
  counters when they hold {1}{U}{U} up; Ambush Wolf / Soul-Shackled Zombie exile their graveyard.
- **BR sacrifice / raid.** Trades and grinds: fodder that leaves value (Infestation Sage {B}, Infernal Vessel {2}{B},
  Reassembling Skeleton, tokens) feeds Vampire Gourmand {1}{B}, Hungry Ghoul {1}{B}, Ravenous Amulet {2}, Arbiter of
  Woe {4}{B}{B}; Involuntary Employment {3}{R} steals a creature to attack, then sacrifice it. Raid after attacking:
  Perforating Artist {1}{B}{R}, Gorehorn Raider {4}{R} (2 damage), Gutless Plunderer {2}{B}, Strongbox Raider
  {2}{R}{R}, Goblin Boarders {2}{R}. Rares: Alesha, High-Society Hunter, Liliana. Against: trade rather than race;
  exile its recursive creatures; respect Burst Lightning and Stab.
- **RG power 4 stompy.** Big bodies early (Llanowar Elves {G}, Ruby {R}{G}, Slumbering Cerberus {1}{R}, Wary Thespian
  {1}{G}, Beast-Kin Ranger {2}{G}, Eager Trufflesnout {2}{G}, Ambush Wolf {2}{G}) turn on Courageous Goblin {1}{R},
  Nessian Hornbeetle {1}{G}, Ruby (+2/+2 attacking); fights and bites as removal (Bite Down {1}{G}, Felling Blow
  {2}{G}, Bushwhack {G}), tricks Sure Strike {1}{R}, Giant Growth {G}, Snakeskin Veil {G}. Rares: Ashroot Animist,
  Twinflame Tyrant, Shivan Dragon. Against: block early, keep the biggest blocker untapped; its removal needs a big
  creature of its own: kill that one first.
- **GW counters go-wide.** One- and two-drops plus +1/+1 counters (Felidar Savior {3}{W}, Treetop Snarespinner
  {3}{G}, Good-Fortune Unicorn {1}{G}{W}, Gnarlid Colony {1}{G}), first strike from Inspiring Paladin {2}{W} on
  creatures with counters during its turn; finishers Overrun, Claws Out (Cats). Rares: Anthem of Champions, Arahbo,
  Sylvan Scavenging, Ajani. Against: keep blockers home; a counter-laden attacker with {G} open may get Snakeskin Veil.
- **WR go-wide aggro.** Cheap creatures and tokens (Hare Apparent {1}{W}, Prideful Parent {2}{W}, Resolute
  Reinforcements {1}{W}, Searslicer Goblin {1}{R}, Goblin Boarders) and team pumps (Heroic Reinforcements {2}{R}{W},
  Goblin Surprise {2}{R}, Dauntless Veteran {1}{W}{W}), finishing with burn (Burst Lightning, Firebrand pings). Rares:
  Swiftblade Vindicator, Kellan. Against: trade early, keep life high; with five open mana expect Heroic
  Reinforcements (two tokens, +1/+1 and haste for the team).
- **UR spells / tempo.** Cheap spells and prowess (Elementalist Adept {1}{U} flash, Heartfire Immolator {1}{R},
  Crackling Cyclops {2}{R}, Firebrand Archer {1}{R}, Balmor {U}{R}), card flow (Think Twice, Thrill of Possibility),
  Tolarian Terror late; removal Burst Lightning {R}, Abrade {1}{R}, Fiery Annihilation {2}{R}. Plays best as control
  with fliers. Rares: Niv-Mizzet, Thousand-Year Storm, Rite of the Dragoncaller. Against: pressure it; its creatures
  are small; kill the pinger.
- **BG morbid midrange.** Deathtouch and trades (Treetop Snarespinner, Gutless Plunderer, Infestation Sage) make
  morbid: Wardens of the Cycle {1}{B}{G}{G} (life or a card each end step), Cackling Prowler {3}{G}, Needletooth Pack
  {3}{G}{G}, Tragic Banshee {4}{B} (-13/-13 if a creature died); Elvish Regrower {2}{G}{G} rebuys. Rares: Spinner of
  Souls, Lathril, Scavenging Ooze. Against: it wins long; avoid trades that turn its morbid cards on in its turn.
- **UG landfall ramp.** The weakest pair: ramp (Llanowar Elves, Grow from the Ashes {2}{G}, Burnished Hart {3}) into
  Tatyova {3}{G}{U} (life and a card per land), Elfsworn Giant {3}{G}{G}, Grappling Kraken {4}{U}{U} (taps and stuns
  per land), Adventuring Gear. Rares: Zimone, Koma, Mossborn Hydra. Against: be faster; it stumbles on tempo.

## Cards to respect (instant speed: tricks, removal, flash). Check against their open mana and colours.
- White: Joust Through {W} 3 damage to an attacker or blocker; Fleeting Flight {W} +1/+1 counter, flying, prevents
  its combat damage; Divine Resilience {W} indestructible (kicked: the whole team); Luminous Rebuke {1}{W} destroy a
  tapped creature ({4}{W} otherwise); Make Your Move {2}{W} destroy power 4+ or an artifact/enchantment; Stroke of
  Midnight {2}{W}; Claws Out (Cats) +2/+2 team; Condemn {W} an attacker to the bottom; flash Cathar Commando {1}{W}
  3/1, Resolute Reinforcements {1}{W}, Mischievous Pup {2}{W}, Twinblade Blessing {1}{W}{W} double strike, Celestial
  Armor {2}{W} hexproof and indestructible.
- Blue: counters Refute {1}{U}{U}, Essence Scatter {1}{U} (creatures), An Offer You Can't Refuse {U} (noncreature);
  Uncharted Voyage {3}{U} tucks a creature; Run Away Together {1}{U} bounces one of each; Faebloom Trick {2}{U} two
  1/1 fliers and taps an attacker or blocker; Aetherize {3}{U} bounces all attackers; Fleeting Distraction {U};
  flash Elementalist Adept {1}{U}, Brineborn Cutthroat {1}{U}, Spectral Sailor {U}.
- Black: Stab {B} -2/-2; Hero's Downfall {1}{B}{B}, Bake into a Pie {2}{B}{B} destroy; Fake Your Own Death {1}{B}
  +2/+0 and returns when it dies.
- Red: Burst Lightning {R} 2 damage (4 kicked, {4}{R}); Abrade {1}{R} 3 damage; Fiery Annihilation {2}{R} 5 damage
  and exile; Sure Strike {1}{R} +3/+0 first strike; Goblin Surprise {2}{R} +2/+0 team or two Goblins; Bulk Up
  {1}{R} double power; Embercleave (cheaper per attacker).
- Green: Giant Growth {G} +3/+3; Snakeskin Veil {G} +1/+1 counter and hexproof; Bite Down {1}{G} (your creature
  deals its power); Broken Wings {2}{G} destroys a flier, artifact or enchantment; Ambush Wolf {2}{G} flash 4/2.
