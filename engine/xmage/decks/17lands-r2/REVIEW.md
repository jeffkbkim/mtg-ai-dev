Made by `python3 play/review_decks.py` (claude-opus-5-5, effort medium, one headless review per deck with no tools; total cost $3.22, replies cached in `review_cache.json`). The reviewer saw the decklist with card text, the curve and counts, but not the record, rank or drafter, so its rating is of the build alone.

- Curve: nonland cards at mana value 1/2/3/4/5/6+ (a Room at its cheaper door, X spells at X=1). Removal: a rough rules-text tag, hard (soft in brackets: bounce, tap-down, can't-block auras).
- Archetype WR: 17lands' win rate for the deck's main colors, all DSK PremierDraft games, splashes combined.
- Red flags: 3+ main colors with fewer than 3 fixing lands; fewer than 5 two-drops or 5+ cards at 6+; lands outside 16-18; archetype WR below 53%.
- Tier 2 (★★, 4 decks): rating >= 4, no red flag, drafter win-rate bucket >= 0.60 (any rank). Every other deck is tier 1 (★). Ratings: 2: 40, 3: 98, 4: 22.
