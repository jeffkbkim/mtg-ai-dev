Archetype: UR artifact tempo/prowess. Cheap threats, baubles and cantrips fill the graveyard to power delirium creatures, metalcraft and Abhorrent Oculus, backed by burn and bounce.

Wins by: Early hasty or flying beats from Swiftspear, DRC, Tamiyo and Bomat Courier. Then a big threat (Abhorrent Oculus, Kappa Cannoneer, Pyrogoyf or a Nettlecyst germ) closes, with Burst Lightning, Galvanic Blast and Legion Extruder burning the last points.

Key cards: Abhorrent Oculus, Dragon's Rage Channeler, Cori-Steel Cutter, Pinnacle Emissary, Kappa Cannoneer, Expressive Iteration, Galvanic Blast, Sink into Stupor, Dig Through Time.

Synergies: Baubles, Spellbomb, Consider and Sleight feed DRC delirium, Pyrogoyf and Oculus (exile 6). The 0-drops trigger prowess and Cutter flurry, and enable Mox Opal and Galvanic metalcraft. Artifacts pump Nettlecyst and Kappa and improvise it out. Emissary makes Drones off artifacts.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 mana sources with a 1-drop threat or Cutter. Mox Diamond needs a spare land to discard. Mox Opal counts as mana only with 2+ other artifacts nearby. Mulligan 1-landers without both Moxen, and hands of all cantrips with no threat. Sink into Stupor can count as a land.

Early turns: T1 DRC, Swiftspear or Tamiyo. Cast the baubles the same turn to surveil and pump prowess. T2 Cutter or Expressive Iteration. Crack fetches early for delirium and fetch Steam Vents first. Hold Spellbomb and Sink for tempo. Cast Oculus once the graveyard holds 6 cards.

Fast mana: Mox Diamond on T1 only if you keep 2+ lands after discarding, ideally a spare Mountain or Island, to cast a 2-drop on T1. Mox Opal: deploy it with baubles to power out a T1-2 Cutter, Emissary or Kappa improvise. Don't strand Opal early with no artifacts.

Against aggro: Burn their early drops with Burst Lightning, Galvanic Blast and Extruder. Tamiyo and Revoker block well. Shadowspear lifelink swings races. Nettlecyst germ and Emissary Drones wall fliers. Oculus as a 5/5 blocker. Don't crack fetches carelessly, and pay 2 life for Steam Vents only when you need tempo.

Against control: Apply pressure fast with haste threats and Bomat Courier refills. Revoker names their key planeswalker or artifact. Shadowspear beats hexproof. Kappa has ward 4. Sink into Stupor answers a sweeper or blocker on the stack. Thieving Skydiver steals a Mox or Equipment. Save Dig Through Time for late fuel. Burn their face to finish.
