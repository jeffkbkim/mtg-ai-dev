Archetype: Green ramp into big threats (Simic splashing black for Pillage the Bog), backed by Nadu value engines and a Craterhoof finish.

Wins by: Ramping out Oko, Nissa or Titania on turns 2-3. Then grinding with Nadu, Tracker and Uro, or ending it with Craterhoof Behemoth or a Nissa-animated land army plus Gaea's Cradle.

Key cards: Oko, Thief of Crowns; Nadu, Winged Wisdom; Green Sun's Zenith; Rofellos; Titania, Protector of Argoth; Craterhoof Behemoth; Nissa, Who Shakes the World; Mana Crypt; Lotus Cobra; Tireless Tracker; Uro.

Synergies: Nadu with Lightning Greaves: re-equip for {0} to different creatures for 2 triggers each turn. Bristly Bill landfall targets and Sylvan Safekeeper also trigger Nadu. Titania with Verdant Catacombs, Prismatic Vista, Strip Mine or Safekeeper sacrifices makes 5/3s. Nissa doubles Forests for Rofellos and Utopia Sprawl.

Mulligan: Keep 7s with 2+ lands plus a mana creature, Sprawl or Crypt, and a payoff. Mulligan no-dork hands of only 5-drops, and 1-landers unless they have Crypt plus a dork. Mana Crypt + Oko or Nadu is a snap keep. Pillage the Bog needs Overgrown Tomb, Gloomlake Verge or Sewers.

Early turns: T1 Birds, Noble or Utopia Sprawl (name green on a Forest). T2 Rofellos > Oko/Nadu > Cobra/Bill. Hold fetchlands to trigger Cobra, Bill and Tracker landfall. Preordain on turn 1 only with no dork. Crack Clues in spare windows.

Fast mana: Always play Mana Crypt turn 1. Crypt + land = turn 1 Oko or Nadu, or Green Sun's Zenith for X=2 to get Rofellos. Gaea's Cradle is late fuel for Hoof or Nissa. GSZ targets: Rofellos early, Titania mid, Craterhoof at 9+ mana for lethal.

Against aggro: Use Oko +1 on their best threat and Sylvan Caryatid or Sentinel as blockers. Uro and Vaultborn Tyrant gain life. GSZ for Sentinel or Titania to stabilize. Don't overload on dorks. Keep Safekeeper up to shroud Nadu or Rofellos.

Against control: Spread threats and favor Oko, Nissa and Nadu over all-in Hoof. Greaves gives shroud and haste vs removal. Strip Mine their key utility land or color. Uro escape and Tracker Clues beat card-advantage wars. GSZ shuffles back in, so it's a late tutor too.
