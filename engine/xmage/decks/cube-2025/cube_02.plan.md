Archetype: WB aggro-midrange: cheap white threats, equipment and token makers, backed by black removal, a little reanimation and planeswalkers. 15 lands plus Mana Crypt and Sol Ring.

Wins by: Going wide early with Adeline, Ajani, Lingering Souls and Luminarch Aspirant, then pulling ahead with Sword of Fire and Ice, Smuggler's Copter, Gideon and Elspeth. Removal clears blockers.

Key cards: Adeline, Resplendent Cathar; Sword of Fire and Ice; Elspeth, Storm Slayer; Gideon, Ally of Zendikar; Luminarch Aspirant; Smuggler's Copter; Demonic Tutor; Get Lost; Fatal Push; Lingering Souls.

Synergies: Elspeth doubles tokens from Lingering Souls, Adeline, Ajani, Gideon's 0 and Ardenvale Fealty. Ajani's Cat token dying flips him into a walker. Copter loots away Takenuma or extra lands. Phelia blinks Splitskin Doll or Deep-Cavern Bat to re-trigger them.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with a 2-drop, or any hand with Sol Ring or Crypt plus a real spell. Mulligan 0-1 landers without fast mana, and 5+ landers with no early play. Fatal Push and a 3-drop alone are too slow.

Early turns: Lead with Figure of Destiny or Deep-Cavern Bat on T1-2, then Aspirant, Ajani or Copter. Adeline on 3. Play Concealed Courtyard in your first three land drops. Keep Get Lost and Push for blockers or key threats. Hold Phelia as a flash ambush or blink.

Fast mana: Crypt or Sol Ring on T1 should power out Adeline, Sword or Gideon on T2, not two 1-drops. Cut Crypt-damage risk by racing. Sol Ring into Sword plus a quick equip is a strong line. Demonic Tutor usually finds Sword, or Elspeth when you're already wide.

Against aggro: Spend Push, Get Lost and Locthwain Scorn early. Block with Preacher and Barrowgoyf (deathtouch). Lifelink from Bat, Sage, Goyf and Elegy swings races. Virtue of Loyalty and Elspeth's -3 stabilize. Tutor for Elspeth or removal. Watch the Crypt flips.

Against control: Spread threats, don't overcommit into sweepers. Lingering Souls flashback, Gideon and Ajani rebuild after a wipe. Bat strips a counterspell or sweeper. Sword (pro-blue) is the top Tutor target. Use Reanimate, Takenuma or Virtue of Persistence to recur killed threats.
