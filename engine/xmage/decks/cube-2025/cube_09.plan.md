Archetype: GW aggro (Naya splash) – white weenie with green mana dorks, Knight of the Reliquary land toolbox and a light red splash for Broadside Bombardiers and Glimmer Lens.

Wins by: Curving one-drops into Adeline, Intrepid Adversary, Luminarch Aspirant and Virtue of Loyalty to go wide and pump. Copter and Spellbinder fly over stalls. Bombardiers and Ajani's 0 burn out the last points.

Key cards: Adeline, Resplendent Cathar; Intrepid Adversary; Virtue of Loyalty; Smuggler's Copter; Knight of the Reliquary; Gaea's Cradle; Mother of Runes; Ajani, Nacatl Pariah; Elite Spellbinder; Voice of Victory.

Synergies: Fetch, then use Knight to fetch Wasteland, Karakas or Cradle; Titania returns fetches and makes 5/3s. Guide of Souls and Enduring Innocence trigger on every token. Ajani's Cat dying flips him. Bombardiers sacrifice Clues, Maps or Copter. Virtue's untap makes Cradle and dorks huge.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with a one-drop or dork plus pressure. Once Upon a Time counts as half a land or creature, so cast it free as your first spell. Ship hands with no plays before turn 3 or with one land and no dork. A dork plus Adeline or Adversary is a snap keep.

Early turns: T1 Noble Hierarch or Birds, otherwise Mother, Luminarch or Guide. T2 Adeline, Copter, Ajani or Knight; T3 Adversary with one pump or Spellbinder to strip their best card. Hold Mother up to protect the key threat, and fetch at the end of their turn to grow Knight.

Fast mana: No Lotus here. Gaea's Cradle is the burst. Save it for a turn that makes a multi-pump Adversary or an early Virtue. Knight fetches Cradle once you have 3+ creatures, and Wasteland once their manabase is greedy. Pay 2 for Stomping Ground only on curve.

Against aggro: Lean on Guide of Souls, Enduring Innocence and Adversary lifelink, plus Sentinel's 3/4 vigilance. Adeline at 0/4+ walls the ground. Mother and Giver protect blockers. Flickerwisp resets a blocker or token. Skip fetch and Canyon life payments when you can.

Against control: Spread threats one or two at a time and keep Mother up against removal. Spellbinder their sweeper or counter, and Voice of Victory blanks instants on your turn. Copter and Glimmer Lens refill after a wipe. Enduring Innocence returns as an enchantment. Witch Enchanter hits their artifacts and enchantments.
