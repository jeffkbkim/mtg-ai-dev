Archetype: Four-color midrange with a Reanimator package: Entomb, Reanimate and Flash into Woodfall Primus or Primeval Titan, backed by Baleful Strix, Leovold, Mana Drain and cheap removal, with Domain enabling a cheap Leyline Binding.

Wins by: Cheating a fat creature (Woodfall Primus, Primeval Titan) out on turns 1-3, or grinding with Strix, Overlord of the Balemurk, Teferi and Kroxa, then closing with Barrowgoyf, Abhorrent Oculus or Esika's Chariot beats.

Key cards: Entomb, Reanimate, Flash, Woodfall Primus, Primeval Titan, Ancestral Recall, Mana Drain, Leyline Binding, Overlord of the Balemurk, Teferi, Hero of Dominaria, Baleful Strix.

Synergies: Flash a Primus for {1}{U}: it kills a noncreature, gets sacrificed, persists back and kills another. Entomb into Primus or Titan, then Reanimate. Impending Overlord mills 4 to fuel Oculus, Kroxa and Goyf. Triomes plus Yavimaya make Binding cost {W} or {1}{W}.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with an early play. Snap keep Entomb+Reanimate, or Flash with Primus/Titan in hand, or Ancestral. Mull 1-landers unless one is a fetch and you hold Cobra. Mull 6-land hands and hands of only 4+ drops.

Early turns: Fetch for duals that cover U/B first; lead with an untapped dual and Entomb (end step) or Strix. Play tapped Triomes/surveil lands on turns with no 1-2 drop. Turn 2 Cobra, Leovold or Flash a fatty. Cast Ancestral on their end step unless you need it now.

Fast mana: No artifact fast mana. Lotus Cobra plus fetches gives bursts: crack a fetch on the same turn for two mana. Spend Mana Drain mana on Teferi, Overlord or Primeval Titan. Titan fetches Yavimaya plus a Triome to max Domain. Cycle Triomes only when flooded.

Against aggro: Mulligan toward interaction: Bone Shards, Sheoldred's Edict, Strix and Barrowgoyf (lifelink). A Reanimated Titan or Primus walls them. Mind the life cost of shocks and fetches. Ouroboroid and Chariot stabilize the board. Save Binding for their best threat.

Against control: Lean on Leovold, Kroxa recursion and Overlord card advantage. Bait counters with Flash or Reanimate before landing Teferi. Hold Mana Drain for their big spell. Use Primus to kill their artifacts and walkers. Entomb at instant speed in their end step to dodge counters.
