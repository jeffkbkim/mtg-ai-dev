Archetype: UB Reanimator/Tinker with a light red splash for Fire Covenant. It plays as an artifact-cheat deck with a removal-heavy midrange backup plan.

Wins by: Cheating Grave Titan or Crabomination in early with Entomb, Reanimate or Persist. Alternatively, Tinker into Portal to Phyrexia. The fallback is grinding with Psychic Frog, Barrowgoyf, Elegy Acolyte and Ertai behind cheap removal.

Key cards: Entomb, Reanimate, Persist, Tinker, Grave Titan, Portal to Phyrexia, Sol Ring, Psychic Frog, Snapcaster Mage, Trinket Mage, Fire Covenant.

Synergies: Entomb Grave Titan, then Reanimate it (lose 6). Persist Crabomination or Titan. Frog or Currency Converter discards a fatty. Snapcaster re-casts Reanimate or Entomb. Takenuma channel returns Titan. Trinket Mage finds Sol Ring. Bauble, Star or Mox Jet feed Tinker and Urza.

Mulligan: Keep 7s with an enabler plus a payoff, such as Entomb+Reanimate or Persist, or Tinker plus an artifact plus 3 mana. Keep 2-3 lands with interaction or Frog. Ship hands that are all fatties, or that have no land and no Sol Ring. Library of Alexandria alone doesn't make a hand keepable.

Early turns: T1 Entomb for Grave Titan at the opponent's end step, then Reanimate on your turn. With only one enabler, hold it until you have the pair. Otherwise lead with Psychic Frog. Hold up Go for the Throat or Dismember. Crack Bauble or Star early for Tinker or Urza value.

Fast mana: Sol Ring plus a land plus Mox Jet on T1 casts Tinker for Portal right away. Tap Sol Ring or Jet for mana first, then sacrifice it to Tinker. Without Tinker, use Sol Ring to cast Urza or Elegy Acolyte early, or to hard-cast Titan on turn 4.

Against aggro: Remove threats first with Snuff Out (free), Dismember, Go for the Throat and Fire Covenant, which can wipe several small creatures. Barrowgoyf and Elegy Acolyte provide lifelink. Watch your life total before paying for Reanimate on Titan, Snuff Out or Covenant. Grave Titan's tokens stabilise the board.

Against control: Bait counterspells with Entomb, then Reanimate at instant speed via Snapcaster. Ertai counters their key spell. Library of Alexandria and Brainstorm keep your hand full. Psychic Frog pressures early. Karakas bounces their legendary creatures. Save Persist for after they use removal.
