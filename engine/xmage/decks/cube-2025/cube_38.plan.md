Archetype: Jeskai tempo-midrange (UWR, no black sources): cheap removal and counters, value threats, Stoneforge into Batterskull, and planeswalkers that take over. Damn is cast only by overload ({2}{W}{W}); Dismember is paid with 4 life.
Wins by: Batterskull on a Germ or another body, Fable value, Nova Hellkite and Quantum Riddler fliers, Comet ticking up, and Colonnade late. Ragavan and Gut pressure early, while Teferi locks the opponent to sorcery speed so your counters and threats stick.
Key cards: Stoneforge Mystic (tutor Batterskull; take Glimmer Lens only if Batterskull is already in hand), Batterskull, Fable of the Mirror-Breaker, Teferi Time Raveler, Ragavan, Bonecrusher Giant, Quantum Riddler, Reckoner Bankbuster, Sol Ring.
Synergies: Stoneforge's {1}{W} ability cheats Batterskull in on turn 3. Batterskull's {3} bounce saves it from removal and replays the Germ. Fable/Gut/Glimmer Lens tokens and Treasures feed Gut's sacrifice. Teferi -3 and Otawara reset blockers. Riddler and Hellkite warp for tempo. Reprieve and Memory Lapse buy turns.
Mulligan: Keep 2-4 landers with a play by turn 2 (Ragavan, Stoneforge, Sol Ring, Talisman, Bankbuster, cheap removal). Ship 1-landers unless you have Sol Ring plus a 1-drop. Ship 5+ lands with no action. Stoneforge in any keepable hand is a strong keep. Need U and R/W sources for your spells.
Early turns: Turn 1 Ragavan, or a tapped land (Colonnade, Spirebluff is fine early). Turn 2 Stoneforge for Batterskull, else Bankbuster or Talisman. Turn 3 activate Stoneforge, or Fable/Bonecrusher/Teferi. Crack Prismatic Vista for whichever basic color is missing. Save Otawara as a land unless you need tempo.
Fast mana: Sol Ring turn 1 enables turn-2 Fable, Bonecrusher or Teferi, and turn-3 hard-cast Batterskull or Riddler. Talisman on 2 into a 4-drop (Comet) on 3. Play the mana rock before a 1-drop if it lets you curve into Batterskull or Stoneforge sooner. Mind the Talisman's pings against aggro.
Against aggro: Spend cheap removal freely (Flame Slash, Galvanic Discharge, Stomp, Dismember). Overload Damn when you are behind on board. Batterskull lifelink is your win condition, so protect it. Hold counters for their bombs only. Hellkite's ping and warp both help stabilize. Avoid paying life on Foundry and Vista when you can.
Against control: Lead with Ragavan and Gut; Teferi stops instant-speed answers and counters. Hold No More Lies, Memory Lapse or Mystic Confluence for their sweepers and walkers. Bankbuster and Fable grind card advantage. Colonnade dodges sorcery removal. Warp Riddler and Hellkite at their end step.
