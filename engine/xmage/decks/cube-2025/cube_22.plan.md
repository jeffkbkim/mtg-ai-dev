Archetype: Mono-red aggro/burn with a light artifact package (Cori-Steel Cutter, Legion Extruder, Clues), 14 Mountains plus Mana Crypt and Mox Pearl.

Wins by: Fast one-drops plus haste threats, then Lightning Bolt, Burst Lightning, Fiery Confluence (6 face damage) and Broadside Bombardiers boasts to finish. Usually kills on turns 4-6.

Key cards: Mana Crypt, Fable of the Mirror-Breaker, Cori-Steel Cutter, Monastery Swiftspear, Dragon's Rage Channeler, Figure of Destiny, Broadside Bombardiers, Fiery Confluence, Lightning Bolt, Pyrogoyf, Carnage Interpreter.

Synergies: Free spells (Probe, Bauble, Manamorphose) trigger Cutter flurry, prowess and DRC surveil. Delirium types fuel DRC, Unholy Heat and Pyrogoyf. Treasures, Clues and Bauble feed Bombardiers, Gut and Extruder. Kiki copies Pyrogoyf for another trigger.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands, or 1 land plus Crypt, with a one-drop or Cutter. Mulligan 0-1 land hands without Crypt, and 5+ landers. With only 14 lands, a 2-lander with cheap spells is a fine keep. Don't keep all-burn hands with no threat.

Early turns: Lead with Swiftspear, DRC or Figure. Make Cutter or Fable your turn-2 play. Hold Bolt and Burst for blockers or the kill, but Flame Slash early utility creatures. Use Probe and Bauble on the same turn as another spell for flurry and prowess. Carnage Interpreter goes down when your hand is nearly empty.

Fast mana: Mana Crypt goes down turn 1 always; the coin-flip damage is irrelevant in a race this fast. Turn-1 Crypt lets you play Fable, Bombardiers or Carnage Interpreter on turn 1-2. Mox Pearl mainly pays Figure's levels; play it early for tempo or hold it as a free artifact for Bombardiers or Extruder.

Against aggro: Be the control deck early. Flame Slash, Unholy Heat and Burst kill their one- and two-drops. Fiery Confluence's 1-damage-to-each-creature mode sweeps tokens. Hired Claw and Swiftspear block well. Figure levels into a 4/4. Skip Probe's life payment and Mana Crypt risk when low.

Against control: Deploy threats one at a time, preferring haste: Swiftspear, Bombardiers, Death-Greeter's Champion dash, Draconautics Engineer. Fable and Interpreter give card advantage. Probe to plan around counters. Save Bolt, Burst and Confluence for the face. Mine Collapse is free on your turn.
