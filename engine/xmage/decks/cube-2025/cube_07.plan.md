Archetype: Grixis Flash/Reanimator. Fast mana and looters cheat Worldspine Wurm, Torsten or Crabomination into play, backed by disruption and Hullbreacher wheel tricks.

Wins by: Flash on Worldspine Wurm, declining to pay, for three 5/5 tramplers. Flash on Torsten for the dig plus seven 1/1s. Or Reanimate a discarded Torsten, Crabomination or Quantum Riddler. Backup: Hullbreacher plus Wheel/Twister, Dack, Jace and Deathrite beats.

Key cards: Flash, Worldspine Wurm, Reanimate, Vampiric Tutor, Jace Vryn's Prodigy, Underworld Breach, Hullbreacher, Wheel of Fortune, Timetwister, Torsten, Crabomination, Lotus Petal, Dark Ritual, Urza's Saga.

Synergies: Wurm is a Flash target only, since it shuffles away from the graveyard. Discard Torsten or Crab to Jace, Dack +1, Vents or Wheel, then Reanimate. Hullbreacher before Wheel/Twister gives you Treasures and starves them. Breach re-escapes Reanimate or Ritual. Saga III fetches Petal/Pearl/Bauble/Spellbomb.

Mulligan: Keep Flash + Wurm/Torsten + U source, or a loot outlet + Reanimate + a fatty. Keep 2-3 lands with Jace/Deathrite and interaction. Mull hands of only fatties and no enablers, or no blue source. Fatties-only is still OK if Vampiric plus mana is present.

Early turns: T1 Deathrite, or Island + Petal/Pearl for Flash on Wurm when you hold it. T2 Jace to loot fatties away, or Bat to strip their answer first. Cast Reanimate T2-3. Pay attention to life: Torsten costs 7, Crab 6. Hold Mana Leak/Ertai to protect the combo turn.

Fast mana: Spend Petal/Pearl only to Flash a fatty, enable an early Hullbreacher-Wheel, or Breach-loop. Don't throw them out for a Bauble. Ritual: Reanimate + Bat/Jace, or Ritual into Fire Covenant. Vampiric for Flash if a fatty is in hand, else for Wurm or Reanimate.

Against aggro: Deathrite, Bat (lifelink), Bloodtithe and Fire Covenant stabilize. Don't Reanimate Torsten at low life; prefer Flash-Wurm or Crab. Torsten dying makes seven blockers. Ertai kills their best threat. Avoid Wheel when they're empty-handed.

Against control: Bat first to take their counter. Combo through Mana Leak or bait with Ertai. Flash at instant speed at their end step. Hullbreacher plus Wheel/Twister wrecks their hand. Use Saga Constructs and Dack to steal artifacts. Jace and Riddler grind long games.
