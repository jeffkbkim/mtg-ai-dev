Archetype: UR artifact tempo-midrange. Fast mana (Moxen, Mana Vault, Ancient Tomb, Urza's Saga) powers out cheap threats and early Karn or Kappa Cannoneer, backed by bounce, cheap removal and card selection.

Wins by: Evasive pressure from Malcolm, Brazen Borrower, Chrome Host Seedshark, Construct tokens and Kappa Cannoneer. Karn and Dack bury the opponent in cards. Tempo plays (Petty Theft, Sink into Stupor, Subtlety) keep blockers and answers off the board.

Key cards: Urza's Saga, Karn, Scion of Urza, Kappa Cannoneer, Third Path Iconoclast, Chrome Host Seedshark, Brazen Borrower, Expressive Iteration, Dack Fayden, Mana Vault, Pyrokinesis, Subtlety.

Synergies: Count artifacts for Saga and Karn Constructs, and to improvise Cannoneer. Cheap noncreature spells (Moxen, Star, Sleight, Spell Pierce) trigger Iconoclast, Seedshark and Cori-Steel Flurry. Dack's +1 discards grow Ivora. Delirium for Unholy Heat comes from artifact, land, instant and sorcery cards.

Mulligan: Keep 2+ lands, or 1 land plus 2 mana rocks, with a 2-drop or Saga. Ship hands of all rocks with no colored source, and 7s with no action. Ancient Tomb plus Vault plus a 4-drop is a snap keep. Mulligan no-landers even if they contain Moxen.

Early turns: Lead with Iconoclast or Ivora on turn 2, then follow with free spells for value. Hold Malcolm, Borrower and Subtlety for flash on the opponent's turn. Play Saga early: at chapter III, fetch Mana Vault for a Karn or Nexus turn, or Mox Sapphire if you need blue. Count lands before casting Consult the Star Charts.

Fast mana: Mana Vault into turn-1 or turn-2 Karn, Kappa Cannoneer, Mightstone or Nexus. Don't Vault for a mere 3-drop. Ancient Tomb is fine for Karn or Dack. Spend Talisman's red only when needed, because it pings you. Use Chromatic Star to fix and draw, ideally with Iconoclast or Seedshark out.

Against aggro: Save Unholy Heat, Mine Collapse (sacrifice a Mountain on your turn) and Pyrokinesis (free by exiling a red card) for two-for-ones. Seedshark is a good 2/4 blocker. Avoid Ancient Tomb and Vault damage. Karn's -2 Constructs block well. Spell Pierce is weak here, so pitch it.

Against control: Deploy Iconoclast and Malcolm early, and resolve Karn or Dack behind Spell Pierce. Flash in threats at end of turn. Sink into Stupor or Petty Theft their walkers. Use Dack -2 to steal key artifacts. Kappa Cannoneer's ward 4 makes it a hard-to-answer finisher. Keep Subtlety for their creature spell or walker.
