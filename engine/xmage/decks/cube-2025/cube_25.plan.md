Archetype: UB reanimator with discard and tutors, splashing red off Steam Vents, Thundering Falls, Riverpyre Verge and Talisman of Creativity for Dack Fayden.

Wins by: Getting Grave Titan, Harvester of Misery or Myr Battlesphere into the graveyard by turns 1-3, returning it with Animate Dead, Necromancy or Persist, then protecting it with discard and Mana Leak.

Key cards: Entomb, Animate Dead, Necromancy, Persist, Grave Titan, Grief, Dark Ritual, Vampiric Tutor, Mystical Tutor, Thoughtseize, Hymn to Tourach.

Synergies: Evoke Grief (exile a black card) to strip their best card, then Animate Dead it back as a 3/2 menace. Other ways to bin a fatty: Bitter Triumph's discard cost, Dack +1, Thundering Falls surveil, Harvester's own discard. Phyrexian Metamorph copying Grave Titan makes two Zombies.

Mulligan: Keep 7s with a reanimation spell plus a way to bin a target (Entomb, Grief, Dack, Bitter Triumph, a Vampiric for either), or two Swamps with discard and interaction. Mulligan land-light hands with no plan, and hands with fatties but no way to return them.

Early turns: Best opener is T1 Swamp, Dark Ritual, Entomb Grave Titan, Animate Dead. Otherwise lead with Thoughtseize or Hymn, then Entomb and reanimate on turn 2. Vampiric Tutor finds the missing piece. Mystical Tutor finds Entomb or Persist. Default Entomb target is Titan; take Battlesphere if they have black removal.

Fast mana: Spend Dark Ritual only to complete a reanimation, a turn-1 Hymn, or an early Grief/Titan. Never Ritual into a lone discard spell on turn 3+. Talisman ramps to turn-3 Dack or turn-4 Harvester; it pings you for U or R, so tap it for colorless when you can.

Against aggro: Use Snuff Out (free, nonblack only) and Bitter Triumph early. Reanimate Grave Titan as a deathtouch wall. Harvester's ETB -2/-2 sweeps go-wide boards but also kills your Tamiyo, Mastermind and Zombies, so cast Harvester before Titan. Skip Thoughtseize and save life.

Against control: Thoughtseize, Hymn or Grief their counters before you commit a reanimation spell. Hold Mana Leak for their sweeper or graveyard hate. Flash Necromancy in response to graveyard exile. Flashing Titan nets two Zombies but Titan dies at cleanup. Dack Fayden and Faerie Mastermind give card advantage.
