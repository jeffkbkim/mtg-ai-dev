Archetype: UR artifact tempo-aggro with a tiny black splash for Booster Tutor. Cheap threats, artifact density for Metalcraft and Urza, cheap interaction, and Skullclamp/Copter card flow.

Wins by: Early pressure from Third Path Iconoclast tokens, Smuggler's Copter, Construct tokens and Kappa Cannoneer. Burn (Galvanic Blast, Abrade, Mine Collapse, Pyrokinesis) clears blockers and finishes; Gut turns spare artifacts into 4/1 menace attackers.

Key cards: Urza's Saga, Urza, Lord High Artificer, Kappa Cannoneer, Skullclamp, Smuggler's Copter, Third Path Iconoclast, Nettlecyst, Galvanic Blast, Pyrokinesis, Mox Opal.

Synergies: Skullclamp on Iconoclast Soldiers or Seasoned Pyromancer Elementals draws 2 each. Bauble, Spellbomb and Moxes enable Metalcraft, grow Constructs and Nettlecyst, and trigger Cannoneer. Iconoclast plus free spells (Probe, Bauble, Daze) makes tokens. Gut can sacrifice a used Bauble.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with a 1-2 drop threat or Saga. Chrome Mox/Opal hands need real follow-ups. Mulligan 1-landers without Saga plus a Mox, and all-cantrip hands with no threat. Iconoclast, Copter or Saga on an otherwise fine 7 is a snap keep.

Early turns: Turn 1 Saga or Iconoclast (with a Mox). Turn 2 Copter, Clamp, or Revoker naming the opponent's best Mox or planeswalker. Saga III fetches Skullclamp if you have a creature, otherwise Mox Opal for Metalcraft. Fetch Watery Grave or Raffine's Tower only when Booster Tutor is in hand.

Fast mana: Chrome Mox goes down turn 1. Imprint a redundant card, like an extra cantrip or Mine Collapse, never Pyrokinesis. Hold Mox Opal until it turns on Metalcraft or adds a free artifact for Cannoneer/Urza. Bauble and Probe are free fuel; cast them before Iconoclast attacks.

Against aggro: Prioritize removal. Pyrokinesis free by exiling a spare red card, Galvanic Blast, Abrade, and Mine Collapse by sacrificing a Mountain on your turn. Revoker and Copter block well. Watch life from Confluence, fetches, Grave and Probe. Urza's 1/4 body and Constructs stabilize.

Against control: Deploy threats one at a time and keep Daze up, best on turns 2-3. Probe first to see their hand. Clamp and Copter grind; Kappa Cannoneer's ward 4 beats spot removal. Wasteland their duals or utility lands. Use Revoker on a planeswalker or Jitte-style threat, and save Saga Constructs as late reloads.
