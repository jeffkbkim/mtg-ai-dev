Archetype: Mono-blue artifact ramp with Tinker (Dismember via Phyrexian mana): fast mana into Urza, Tezzeret and Tinker for a big threat, backed by Force of Will, Daze and Miscalculation.
Wins by: Tinker for Portal to Phyrexia (opponent has creatures) or Myr Battlesphere (empty board), or hard-cast via Workshop/Grim Monolith/Mana Vault. Batterskull, Nettlecyst, Urza's Construct and Seedshark tokens win slower games.
Key cards: Tinker, Mishra's Workshop, Mana Vault, Grim Monolith, Ancestral Recall, Urza, Lord High Artificer, Tezzeret, Cruel Captain, Force of Will, Portal to Phyrexia, Myr Battlesphere, Batterskull.
Synergies: Tinker can sac Bauble or Chromatic Star (Star still draws). Tezzeret -3 finds Mana Vault or a 1-drop, and his 0 untaps Vault/Monolith. Urza taps artifacts for U. Metamorph copies Battlesphere/Batterskull. Workshop casts Batterskull or Battlesphere on turn 2-3.
Mulligan: Keep 2+ lands (or land + Vault/Monolith/Workshop) with a payoff like Tinker, Urza, Tezzeret or a fatty plus a cantrip. Mull hands that are all lands, or all cheap artifacts with no payoff. Best-of-one: keep a 7 with Tinker + artifact + 2 blue sources.
Early turns: Turn 1: land, Bauble/Star/Lantern/Converter, or Tamiyo; Ponder to dig for Tinker. Turn 2: Seedshark, or Monolith into a 3-drop. Turn 3: Tinker (Portal if they have 2+ creatures, else Battlesphere), or Urza/Tezzeret. Use Strand early to thin and fix.
Fast mana: Spend Mana Vault/Monolith only for Tinker, Urza, Tezzeret or a fatty, not on cantrips. Workshop is artifacts only: aim it at Batterskull, Battlesphere, Metamorph or Nettlecyst. Untap Vault only when spare mana; Tezzeret 0 untaps it for free.
Against aggro: Dismember their best early threat (pay life only if healthy). Tamiyo and Seedshark block. Rush Batterskull via Workshop/Tinker. Portal is the reset button. Daze their turn-1/2 plays; save Force of Will for a big swing spell or anthem.
Against control: Bait counters with Urza/Tezzeret before Tinker, or Tinker with Daze/Force backup. Prefer Battlesphere vs spot removal. Ancestral at their end of turn. Channel Otawara late. Converter and Foundry grind; Lantern exiles their recursion targets.
