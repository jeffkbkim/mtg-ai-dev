Archetype: Gx lands-ramp (Gruul/Golgari splash) that uses mana elves and landfall to cast Minsc & Boo, Primeval Titan, Titania and Ugin early, with a Crucible of Worlds plus Strip Mine land-destruction engine.

Wins by: Ramping into Minsc & Boo, Primeval Titan or Ouroboroid on turn 2-3, then turning landfall (Scythecat Cub, Titania tokens, Restless Ridgeline) into big trampling attacks. Ugin or a Crucible plus Strip Mine lock closes long games.

Key cards: Minsc & Boo, Primeval Titan, Titania, Protector of Argoth, Ouroboroid, Ugin, Eye of the Storms, Crucible of Worlds, Strip Mine, Lotus Cobra, Scythecat Cub, Wishclaw Talisman, Utopia Sprawl, Exploration.

Synergies: Fetches + Titania make 5/3s. Crucible replays fetches and Strip Mine every turn. Prime Titan fetches Strip Mine + Bloodstained Mire. Reclaimer finds Strip/Ridgeline. Cobra/Scythecat + Exploration double landfall. Minsc +1 or Scythecat counters grow Ouroboroid. Mycospawn/Talisman/Crucible trigger Ugin.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands plus a 1-drop accelerant (Llanowar Elves, Elvish Mystic, Birds, Utopia Sprawl) and one payoff. Mulligan hands with no green source, all payoffs and no ramp, or 6+ lands without Exploration or Crucible. One land plus two elves is a keep on the play.

Early turns: T1 Utopia Sprawl on a Forest (choose red) or an elf; lead with Sprawl if the opponent might have removal. T2 Lotus Cobra, Scythecat or Wishclaw. Hold fetches to crack with Cobra or Scythecat for extra landfall. Aim for Minsc or Ouroboroid on T3, Titan on T4.

Fast mana: Hold Manamorphose to fix colors or to complete a Titan or Minsc turn; it replaces itself. Use Wishclaw only when the found card wins or locks: Primeval Titan or Minsc on a stable board, Crucible if you have Strip Mine. It goes to the opponent after one use, so never activate it casually.

Against aggro: Barrowgoyf (lifelink, deathtouch) and Sentinel (3/4 vigilance) hold the ground. Minsc -2 by sacrificing a pumped Boo kills their best creature. Pest Infestation answers artifacts and makes chump blockers. Skip Duress and Strip Mine early; cast blockers before greedy ramp.

Against control: Surrak can't be countered and draws when targeted, so lead with it. Duress before the big turn. Strip Mine, Boseiju channel and kicked Sowing Mycospawn cut their mana. Bait counters with Titania before Titan. Minsc makes Boo every upkeep and the Crucible+Strip lock grinds them; Ugin +2 refuels.
