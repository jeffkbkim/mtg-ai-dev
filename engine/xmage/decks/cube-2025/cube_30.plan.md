Archetype: White Weenie aggro splashing blue for Time Walk, Snapcaster and cantrips. Dismember is cast with life, so the deck is effectively mono-W with a light blue touch.

Wins by: Curving one- and two-drops into an early board, then pumping it with Intrepid Adversary, Luminarch Aspirant, Gideon's emblem or Jitte. Carry the damage in the air with Smuggler's Copter, Serra Paragon and Celestial Colonnade.

Key cards: Umezawa's Jitte, Smuggler's Copter, Mother of Runes, Giver of Runes, Ocelot Pride, Luminarch Aspirant, Intrepid Adversary, Gideon Ally of Zendikar, Time Walk, Thalia Guardian of Thraben, Voice of Victory, Dismember.

Synergies: Mother and Giver protect the Jitte or Aspirant carrier. Voice and Glimmer Lens tokens turn Lens draws on. Copter loots away extra lands. Snapcaster rebuys Time Walk, Dismember or March. Paragon replays Strip Mine or a two-drop. Cosmogrand triggers off cheap double-spell turns.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with at least one W source and a one- or two-drop. Ship hands with no plays before turn 3 or with blue-only mana. A one-lander with Ponder or Preordain plus two one-drops is a keep on the play. Jitte or Copter plus Mother is a snap keep.

Early turns: T1 Mother, Ocelot Pride, Aspirant-curve or Thraben Inspector. Pick Thalia over a cantrip against spells. T2 Copter or Aspirant, then Adversary or Blade Splicer on 3. Play Bauble for free whenever. Save Ponder and Preordain for land-light hands. Fetch nothing. Pay Passage life only to curve out.

Fast mana: There is no rock or Lotus, so Time Walk is the burst. Cast it on turn 2-3 after landing a threat, for an extra attack plus land drop. Strip Mine their only blue or red source on turn 1-2, or a manland or Library. Use Mana Confluence for Dismember with Snapcaster or Time Walk mana.

Against aggro: Mother and Giver block-protect; first strikers (Ocelot, Thalia, Golem) hold the ground. Jitte is the top priority: -1/-1 kills their x/1s, and lifegain matters. Dismember their best threat. Kicker Adversary later as a big lifelinker. Don't pay life on Passage or Confluence carelessly.

Against control: Overload early threats under Thalia, and cast Voice to stop instant-speed answers on your turn. Hold Mother or Giver mana to protect a threat from removal. Gideon and Colonnade survive sweepers. Keep one or two creatures in hand against Wrath. Flash in Snapcaster at end of turn. Strip Mine their colour-screwed land.
