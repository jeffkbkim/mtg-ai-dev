Archetype: Green ramp and creatures (Gx with B/R/W splashes). Mana dorks and Cradle into Natural Order, Ouroboroid, Vaultborn Tyrant, and the planeswalkers Grist and Wrenn and Six.

Wins by: Ramping out a fast threat, then snowballing. Ouroboroid pumps the whole board each combat. Natural Order puts Vaultborn Tyrant into play. Grist makes chump-proof Insects. Laelia and Broadside Bombardiers add haste beats and burn reach.

Key cards: Natural Order, Vaultborn Tyrant, Ouroboroid, Rofellos, Gaea's Cradle, Elvish Reclaimer, Urza's Saga, Grist, Wrenn and Six, Leyline Binding, Valki.

Synergies: Natural Order sacrifices Elvish Mystic, Rofellos or Fanatic to fetch Vaultborn Tyrant. Reclaimer and Wight fetch Cradle, Saga or Karakas. Wrenn and Six's +1 returns a used Verdant Catacombs. Saga III grabs Mox Emerald or Haywire Mite. Ouroboroid's counters turn on Fanatic's GGGG.

Mulligan: Keep 2+ lands with a green source plus a dork, or Mox plus a dork. Mull 7s with no green mana or no early play. A 1-landers is OK only with Mox Emerald plus Elvish Mystic and a payoff. Natural Order hands need a green creature to sacrifice.

Early turns: T1 Elvish Mystic, Giver or Ocelot Pride (Mox first if you have it). T2 Rofellos when you have 2+ Forests, else Fanatic or Caryatid. T3-4 Natural Order for Tyrant, Ouroboroid, or a walker. Play Commercial District and the Triomes tapped on turns you have spare mana.

Fast mana: Drop Mox Emerald immediately. Play Gaea's Cradle once you have 2+ creatures. Reclaimer can fetch it later. Spend Malevolent Rumble's Spawn and Cradle bursts on Natural Order or Tyrant, not on small spells. Saga II makes Constructs only with spare mana.

Against aggro: Lead with Giver, Caryatid and Fanatic (1/4) as walls. Wrenn's -1 pings X/1s. Grist's -2 kills attackers. Haywire Mite gains life. Ocelot Pride and Tyrant gain life. Use Leyline Binding at flash speed on their best threat. Don't over-sacrifice your blockers.

Against control: Spread threats rather than going all in. Hold Giver to protect a key creature from removal. Valki strips their creature plan. Lead with walkers and hasty Laelia or Bombardiers. Haywire Mite exiles their artifacts and enchantments. Karakas bounces legends. Cast Natural Order when they're tapped out.
