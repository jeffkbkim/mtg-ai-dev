Archetype: Rakdos aggro-midrange with a discard engine (Pyromancer, Tersa, FOMO, Interpreter, Inti) and a small Reanimate package. It plays cheap threats and burn, then tops out with Glorybringer and Fury.

Wins by: Early pressure from 2-drops backed by burn and removal, then haste and evasion finishers. Glorybringer exerts each turn, Fury double strikes, Tersa has haste. Reanimate or Emperor of Bones can cheat a discarded Fury or Crabomination in early.

Key cards: Glorybringer, Fury, Carnage Interpreter, Seasoned Pyromancer, Bloodtithe Harvester, Fire Covenant, Reanimate, Emperor of Bones, Mana Vault, Mox Ruby, Galvanic Discharge, Caustic Bronco.

Synergies: Discarding Fury or Crabomination to Pyromancer, Tersa, FOMO, Inti or Interpreter sets up a turn-2 Reanimate. Discard also grows Ivora and triggers Inti. A varied graveyard grows Pyrogoyf and turns on FOMO delirium. Interpreter's Clues feed Crabomination emerge. Cutter makes Monks off a second spell each turn.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands, or 1 land plus Mox Ruby, with a 1-2 drop and some interaction. Mull 7-card hands with no plays before turn 3, all top-end, or 5+ lands. A hand of Reanimate + discard outlet + fat target is a keep even with only 2 lands.

Early turns: Turn 1: Hired Claw or Mox plus a 2-drop. Turn 2: Harvester, Bronco, Ivora, Robber, Emperor or FOMO. Turn 3: Interpreter after dumping your hand, or Pyromancer to pitch big spells. Crack fetches early for Pyrogoyf land types. Hold Fire Covenant and Mine Collapse for blockers.

Fast mana: Mox Ruby: play it turn 1, always. Mana Vault: use it for a turn-2 Glorybringer, Fury or Interpreter plus a 2-drop, or a turn-1 3-drop. Don't untap it unless you have nothing else to do; take the pings. Avoid turn-1 Vault into nothing.

Against aggro: Lead with removal over racing. Burst Lightning, Galvanic Discharge and Sheoldred's Edict handle early drops. Fury (evoke by exiling a red card if needed) and Fire Covenant are blowouts. Harvester trades up, FOMO walls the ground. Pay Covenant life carefully. Glorybringer stabilizes.

Against control: Deploy two threats at most, then hold backups like Tersa, Robber, Bronco and Dash Champion. Interpreter refills you through sweepers. Keep burn to the face to finish. Bronco and Inti generate card advantage. Use Crabomination late to strip their hand. Save Edict for their finisher.
