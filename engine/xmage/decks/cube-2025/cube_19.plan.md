Archetype: Rakdos graveyard midrange with a reanimator package. Cheap removal and graveyard threats carry most games; Entomb plus Death or Emperor of Bones can cheat in Archon of Cruelty or Grave Titan early.

Wins by: Reanimating Archon of Cruelty or Grave Titan on turns 1-3, or grinding with Kroxa escapes, Nethergoyf, Fable of the Mirror-Breaker and Elegy Acolyte card draw, while Bolt, Push, Fire Covenant and Pyrokinesis clear blockers.

Key cards: Entomb, Life // Death, Emperor of Bones, Archon of Cruelty, Grave Titan, Fable of the Mirror-Breaker, Kroxa, Fire Covenant, Faithless Looting, Black Lotus. Entomb's first pick is Archon; take Grave Titan only if Archon is drawn or already used.

Synergies: Looting, Fable chapter II and Inti discards bin fatties for Death or Emperor. Emperor exiles a creature at combat; pay {1}{B} adapt for a hasty one with ETB and attack triggers. Fetches and spells grow DRC, Nethergoyf and Pyrogoyf.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 landers with a 1-2 drop or removal. Snap-keep Entomb or Looting with Death or Emperor of Bones. Mulligan hands without black, and seven-card hands holding Archon or Titan with no way to discard or reanimate them.

Early turns: Lead with DRC, Nethergoyf or Looting on turn 1, then Emperor of Bones or Inti on turn 2. Cast Entomb at the opponent's end step. Fetch for Blood Crypt or Swamp early; Death costs {1}{B}. Keep the Fable as your turn-3 play.

Fast mana: Spend Black Lotus for a turn-1 kill setup: Swamp, Lotus for BBB, Entomb Archon, then Death. Otherwise save it to slam Grave Titan or Archon a few turns early. Never use it just for two-drops. Use Arena of Glory's exert to give Nova Hellkite or Kroxa haste.

Against aggro: Kill early with Bolt, Push, Lavamancer and free Pyrokinesis. Fire Covenant swings the board; pay life only while you are safe. Elegy Acolyte's lifelink stabilizes. Reanimate Grave Titan over Archon if you need blockers. Pay 2 life for Blood Crypt and Passage sparingly.

Against control: Hold Entomb and Death until a counter is tapped out, or use instant-speed Entomb on their end step. Emperor of Bones dodges sorcery-speed answers. Kroxa escapes, Fable and Fiery Confluence's 6 face damage finish games. Keep Bolt for the last points.
