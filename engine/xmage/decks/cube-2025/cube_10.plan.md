Archetype: Red-based Mardu midrange-aggro with small splashes: Teferi (U) and Bloodbraid Elf / Minsc & Boo (G). Cheap red threats, efficient removal, value creatures and planeswalkers on top.
Wins by: Early pressure from Dragon's Rage Channeler, Voldaren Epicure, Generous Plunderer and Inti. Then you take over with Fable, Adeline, Screaming Nemesis, Minsc & Boo and Goldspan Dragon, using burn (Ghostfire Slice, Galvanic Discharge) to close.
Key cards: Fable of the Mirror-Breaker, Minsc & Boo, Adeline, Goldspan Dragon, Screaming Nemesis, Solitude, Teferi Time Raveler, Bloodbraid Elf, Galvanic Discharge, Fatal Push.
Synergies: Inti plus Fable chapter II or Collective Brutality turns discards into card advantage. Minsc's -2 on Boo, or on a +1'd Boo, draws 4+. Treasures from Goldspan and Plunderer fuel Detective's Phoenix and Emperor of Bones. Serra Paragon recasts Epicure, Inti or Fable.
Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with red mana and at least one 1-2 drop or removal spell. Mulligan hands with no red source, and 5+ landers without Fable or Minsc. Do not keep hands that need both the U and G splash to function.
Early turns: Lead with Dragon's Rage Channeler or Epicure on turn 1, then Plunderer or Inti on turn 2. Hold Push and Discharge for real threats. Fetch Taiga with Wooded Foothills if Bloodbraid or Minsc is in hand. Play Blood Crypt and Temple Garden untapped only if it adds a play that turn.
Fast mana: No artifact fast mana here. Goldspan treasures give two mana each, so spend them on a turn-6 Solitude hardcast plus a burn spell. Spend Plunderer treasures to ramp into a turn-3 Minsc & Boo or a 4-mana Goldspan. Cycle Raffine's Tower and Jetmir's Garden late.
Against aggro: Use early Push and Discharge on mana dorks and 1-2 drops. Brutality's -2/-2 doubles as lifegain. Adeline and Serra Paragon wall the ground and air. Evoke Solitude (pitch Reprieve or Glimmer Lens) to stop a big threat. Screaming Nemesis punishes burn and blocks.
Against control: Deploy threats one at a time and keep haste for after sweepers: Nemesis, Goldspan, a dashed Death-Greeter's Champion, Bloodbraid. Brutality strips a counter or sweeper. Teferi shuts off instant-speed answers. Reprieve protects your key spell. Minsc and Fable grind card advantage.
