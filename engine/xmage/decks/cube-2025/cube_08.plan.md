Archetype: Esper (WUB) midrange with a Flash + Worldspine Wurm combo and a small reanimation package. Cheap removal, Mana Leak and No More Lies, and efficient threats like Barrowgoyf, Preacher of the Schism and Elegy Acolyte.
Wins by: Flash on a Worldspine Wurm in hand: don't pay, sacrifice it, get three 5/5 tramplers for 2 mana. Otherwise grind with Preacher, Barrowgoyf, Elegy Acolyte and Teferi, Hero of Dominaria behind removal and counters. Celestial Colonnade closes late.
Key cards: Flash, Worldspine Wurm, Mystical Tutor (finds Flash, or Path to Exile/Fatal Push in a pinch), Teferi, Hero of Dominaria, Barrowgoyf, Preacher of the Schism, Necromancy, Crabomination, Fatal Push, Path to Exile.
Synergies: Wurm shuffles back from any graveyard, so only Flash it from hand. Never reanimate it, and Emperor's finality counter exiles it. Use Reanimate, Necromancy and Death on Crabomination, Acolyte or the foe's best creature. Splitskin Doll's discard and surveil lands stock the yard.
Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with interaction plus a threat, or any hand with Flash/Mystical Tutor + Worldspine Wurm and blue mana. Mulligan 0-1 landers (Dark Ritual + Bauble is not mana), 6-landers, and hands of only reanimation spells with nothing to bring back.
Early turns: T1 Fatal Push or Path on their first threat, or hold up Mystical Tutor. T2 Emperor of Bones or Splitskin Doll, or keep Mana Leak/Phelia mana open. T3 Preacher or Barrowgoyf. Fetch to Underground Sea/Scrubland early. Hold No More Lies for their key spell.
Fast mana: Dark Ritual is one-shot. Spend it T1 on Preacher, Barrowgoyf or Necromancy on a binned Crabomination, or on Flash + Wurm a turn early with U up. Don't waste it on 1-drops. Crack Bauble early to fuel the goyfs.
Against aggro: Trade with Push, Path and Oust early. Preacher and Barrowgoyf block well with deathtouch, and Barrowgoyf and Acolyte gain life. Don't Reanimate expensive targets at low life. Flash-Wurm makes three 5/5 blockers; fire it at instant speed in their combat.
Against control: Bait counters with Preacher and Barrowgoyf, then Flash the Wurm at their end step or in response to tapping out. Hold Mana Leak/No More Lies to protect Teferi. Phelia at instant speed dodges sorcery removal. Use Consult the Star Charts kicked late, and Colonnade against wraths.
