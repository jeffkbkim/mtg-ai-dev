Archetype: 4-color (Bant + black, red splash) domain ramp-midrange: mana dorks and Oko early, removal plus sweepers in the middle, Natural Order/Atraxa and Omnath as top end.

Wins by: Oko elking threats while making Food, Uro and Psychic Frog card advantage, then Atraxa (hard-cast or Natural Order), Elspeth tokens, Esika's Chariot, Ouroboroid or Quantum Riddler closing in the air.

Key cards: Oko, Thief of Crowns; Natural Order (fetch Atraxa); Uro; Swords to Plowshares; Toxic Deluge; Sunfall; Leyline Binding; Fanatic of Rhonas; Sylvan Caryatid; Atraxa, Grand Unifier.

Synergies: Natural Order sacs Caryatid/Fanatic/cat token for Atraxa. Ouroboroid fixes Fanatic's ferocious for GGGG. Elspeth doubles Chariot cats/Food. Domain (Triome, Archive, Falls, Mortuary, Plateau) makes Leyline Binding 1-2 mana. Frog discard plus Rumble/surveil fuel Uro escape.

Mulligan: Keep 7s with 3+ lands, or 2 lands plus Caryatid/Fanatic/Mox Pearl, with green access. Ship hands of all 5-6 drops or no green source. Once Upon a Time on the opener counts as a free land/dork find. Mull to 6 freely; 5-color mana needs lands.

Early turns: Cast Once Upon a Time first if it's in hand (free). Play taplands/Triome on turns 1 and 4 when idle. T2 Caryatid/Fanatic/Frog, T3 Oko or Uro (land ramp). Fetch Arid Mesa into Plateau. Keep Phelia and Swords up at instant speed. Rumble when nothing better.

Fast mana: Mox Pearl is permanent. Use it T1 for a T2 three-drop (Oko/Uro off Pearl + 2 lands with green dork), or to cast Swords while still developing. Don't hold it; it's also Leyline Binding's cheap enabler only via W. Fanatic ferocious bursts into Atraxa.

Against aggro: Prioritize Swords, Dismember, Fire Covenant early. Caryatid/Fanatic as blockers, Barrowgoyf lifelink stabilizes. Toxic Deluge/Sunfall when behind on board. Uro and Omnath gain life. Oko +1 their best creature. Don't greedily Natural Order before stabilizing.

Against control: Lead with Frog and Oko to pressure early, hold Fractured Identity/Leyline Binding for their planeswalkers or key permanents. Uro escape is counter-resilient. Natural Order for Atraxa is the big swing; bait counters with Riddler/Elspeth first. Karakas is utility.
