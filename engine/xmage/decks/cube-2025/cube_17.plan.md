Archetype: WUB Esper midrange with a reanimator package. Grind with cheap interaction, card flow and value creatures, then cheat Griselbrand in with Animate Dead or Unburial Rites when he lands in the graveyard.

Wins by: Lifelink and evasive pressure (Deep-Cavern Bat, Preacher of the Schism, Lingering Souls, Abhorrent Oculus, Chrome Host Seedshark) after stripping their answers. Alternatively, reanimate Griselbrand, draw 7 and win from there.

Key cards: Griselbrand, Animate Dead, Unburial Rites, Grief, Abhorrent Oculus, Preacher of the Schism, Toxic Deluge, Fractured Identity, Lingering Souls, Fatal Push, No More Lies.

Synergies: Bin Griselbrand with Bone Shards (discard mode), Currency Converter (decline the exile) or surveil (Consider, Archive, Backstreet). Evoke Grief, then Animate Dead it for two discards and a 2/2 menace. Fetches and Consider fuel Oculus. Only one of Oculus or Murderous Cut gets the graveyard.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 mana sources (Moxen count) with interaction or a threat. Snap-keep Mox plus Grief or Animate Dead, or Griselbrand plus a discard outlet. Mulligan 0-1 landers without a Mox and hands of only clunky 4-5 drops. Booster Tutor is not a plan.

Early turns: T1 Inquisition of Kozilek or Consider. T2 Deep-Cavern Bat, Caustic Bronco or Sorin. T3 Preacher of the Schism or Seedshark. Fetch early to stock the graveyard for Oculus. Hold Fatal Push and No More Lies when the opponent is likely to act. Pay shockland life only when the mana matters.

Fast mana: Spend a Mox on turn 1 for Mox Jet + land: evoke Grief, then Animate Dead it. Otherwise use it for a turn-2 three-drop or Preacher. Keep Mox Jet for Unburial Rites plus Bone Shards or Fatal Push in the same turn. Strip Mine their key utility land or a two-lander.

Against aggro: Fatal Push, Bone Shards and Toxic Deluge are your answers; Deluge for a small X once they commit 3+ creatures. Wall them with Preacher's deathtouch and Sorin's 1/4 body. Gain life with the Bat and Souls. Skip Inquisition. Hardcast Griselbrand only once stable.

Against control: Lead with Inquisition, Grief and the Bat to strip counters, then land Griselbrand via Animate Dead at instant-safe windows. Unburial Rites has flashback, so it beats one counter. Back threats with No More Lies. Grind with Lingering Souls, Bronco and Stock Up. Fractured Identity their planeswalker. Strip Mine a land.
