Archetype: Grixis artifact ramp: fast mana and Tolarian Academy into big colorless threats, cheapened by Tinker or improvise, with cheap red removal and Toxic Deluge.
Wins by: Tinker for Portal to Phyrexia or Myr Battlesphere, a huge Kappa Cannoneer, or Ugin, Eye of the Storms exiling their board. Small backup: Ragavan, Nettlecyst, Pinnacle Emissary drones, and Urza or Karn constructs.
Key cards: Tinker, Demonic Tutor, Wishclaw Talisman, Tolarian Academy, Urza, Lord High Artificer, Portal to Phyrexia, Myr Battlesphere, Kappa Cannoneer, Ugin, Eye of the Storms, Tezzeret, Cruel Captain, the Moxen.
Synergies: Tinker sacrificing a Mox gets Portal (vs creatures) or Battlesphere. Academy, Urza and Candelabra turn artifact counts into burst mana. Ugin's cast trigger repeats on every colorless spell. Tezzeret -3 finds Mox Opal or Candelabra. Emissary makes a drone per artifact.
Mulligan: Keep 2+ mana sources with an early play. Snap keep Tinker plus any artifact, or Academy plus Moxen/Talismans. Mull hands of only 7-drops, or a no-land hand with only Mox Emerald (it is just colorless here). Keep a 6 with a tutor and lands.
Early turns: Lead with lands, Moxen and Ragavan (dash if they have blockers). Then a Talisman, Legion Extruder pinging a creature, or Currency Converter. Hold Bolt and Galvanic Discharge for real threats. Turn 2-3, Tinker or Demonic Tutor into Tinker. Save Wishclaw for a finished board, since they get it back.
Fast mana: Play Moxen right away; they feed Academy, Opal metalcraft, improvise and Nettlecyst. Spend Talismans on turn-2 Tinker or turn-3 Urza/Karn/Tezzeret. Academy plus Candelabra or Urza can hard-cast Battlesphere or Ugin. Don't sacrifice your only color source to Tinker.
Against aggro: Use Bolt, Galvanic Discharge and Legion Extruder early. Toxic Deluge resets a wide board (your artifacts survive). Portal to Phyrexia is the best Tinker target, then Battlesphere for blockers. Use Urza's construct and Nettlecyst germ to block. Mind Talisman and Fiery Islet life loss.
Against control: Duress first to clear a counter, then Tinker or Kappa Cannoneer (ward 4, unblockable). Ragavan and Bankbuster add pressure and cards. Fight with planeswalkers: Karn +1, Tezzeret. Ugin's cast trigger beats counters. Demonic Tutor for Tinker or Ugin.
