Archetype: UB tempo-midrange with a light white splash (Lingering Souls only). Cheap interaction, evasive threats and flash creatures, backed by fast mana that powers out Karn, Ertai and Grave Titan.

Wins by: Chip damage from evasive threats (Deep-Cavern Bat, Thieving Skydiver, Psychic Frog, Lingering Souls, Qarsi Revenant) while you answer their plays. Turn hits into cards with Enduring Curiosity or Fallen Shinobi. Close with Grave Titan or Karn constructs.

Key cards: Psychic Frog, Enduring Curiosity, Fallen Shinobi, Grave Titan, Ertai Resurrected, Karn Scion of Urza, Subtlety, Mana Crypt, Sol Ring. Removal suite: Fatal Push, Go for the Throat, Bitter Triumph.

Synergies: Ninjutsu Fallen Shinobi off an unblocked flier. Karakas bounces your own Ertai to reuse its ETB. Fetch or Wasteland enables Push revolt and feeds Deathrite. Frog discards fuel Woe Strider's escape. Artifacts pump Karn constructs. Hearse eats graveyards; Spirits crew it.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands, or 1 land plus Crypt/Sol Ring/Ancient Tomb and a castable play. Want a black source plus a 1-2 drop or removal. Ship hands of all top end, and 0-1 landers without fast mana. Don't keep for white; Lingering Souls is a bonus.

Early turns: T1 Inquisition (take their best 3-drop or fast mana), Deathrite or Bauble. T2 Frog or Bat; Skydiver kicked if they show artifacts. Hold Memory Lapse and Push up rather than tapping out. Crack Misty for Underground Sea. Wasteland their duals or utility lands.

Fast mana: Crypt/Sol Ring/Tomb on T1 into a 3-4 drop: Qarsi Revenant, Woe Strider, Karn, or flash Curiosity/Ertai held up. Crypt plus Tomb plus a land enables a T2 Grave Titan; do it. Crypt costs life, so vs aggro deploy blockers first. Steal their Sol Ring/Crypt with Skydiver X=1.

Against aggro: Go removal-heavy: Push, Go for the Throat, Bitter Triumph (pay life only when safe). Block with Woe Strider plus Goat and Lingering Souls. Lifelink from Bat and Revenant races. Ertai kills on flash. Grave Titan stabilizes. Mind your life with Crypt, Tomb and shocks.

Against control: Lead with Inquisition and Bat to strip counters. Deploy flash threats (Curiosity, Subtlety, Tidebinder, Ertai) at their end step. Memory Lapse their sweeper or walker. Grind with Karn, Frog cards, Lingering Souls flashback, Woe Strider escape and Wasteland on their utility lands.
