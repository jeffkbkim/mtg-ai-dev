Archetype: Esper-splash (really WU) artifact tempo with a Tinker package: cheap white creatures and disruption up front, plus Tinker or Grim Monolith into Myr Battlesphere, Portal to Phyrexia or Coveted Jewel.
Wins by: Chip damage from Elite Spellbinder, Sage of the Skies, Phelia and Thieving Skydiver, protected by Spell Pierce and No More Lies. Then an artifact bomb ends it: Tinker into Myr Battlesphere or Portal to Phyrexia, or hardcast off Grim Monolith. Celestial Colonnade and Saga Constructs close late.
Key cards: Tinker, Ancestral Recall, Grim Monolith, Urza's Saga, Myr Battlesphere, Portal to Phyrexia, Elite Spellbinder, Narset, Parter of Veils, No More Lies, Dismember, March of Otherworldly Light.
Synergies: Tinker can sacrifice Grim Monolith, Retrofitter Foundry, Esper Sentinel, Thraben Inspector's Clue, Servos or Saga Constructs. Urza's Saga chapter III fetches Retrofitter Foundry, or Esper Sentinel as early pressure. Thieving Skydiver kicked steals their Moxen or Sol Ring. Narset -2 digs for Tinker or Ancestral.
Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with a 1-2 drop or interaction. Snap keep Tinker plus any artifact plus 3 lands, or Grim Monolith plus a 6-7 drop. Ship hands with one land and no Monolith or Saga, and 5-land hands with only top-end. Ponder and Probe count as half a card, not as lands.
Early turns: T1 Thraben Inspector or Esper Sentinel, or Ponder to find Tinker or lands. T2 Phelia or Cathar Commando at instant speed, or Grim Monolith. T3 Tinker if you have an artifact, else Elite Spellbinder to take their best card. Cast Ancestral at the end of their turn.
Fast mana: Grim Monolith on T2 means T3 Coveted Jewel (draw 3), or T4 Myr Battlesphere and T5 Portal. Don't hardcast Jewel into a board that can attack unblocked. Prefer Tinker with Monolith as the sacrifice once the bomb is better than the mana. Saga gives colorless mana plus a fetch.
Against aggro: Lead with Thraben Inspector and Sage of the Skies (lifelink). Hold Dismember (pay 4 life, no black source) and Oust for their best threat. Tinker into Portal to Phyrexia wipes three creatures, and Battlesphere walls the ground. Don't tap low for Jewel. Karakas bounces legendary attackers.
Against control: Play Esper Sentinel first, then Elite Spellbinder and Gitaxian Probe to strip and see answers. Hold Spell Pierce or No More Lies to protect Tinker and bombs. Narset shuts off their draw-sevens and Brainstorm. Use Loran or Cathar Commando on their artifacts and enchantments, and steal Moxen with Thieving Skydiver.
