Archetype: Boros (R/W) aggro. A low curve of white weenies plus red burn and value creatures, with Skullclamp as the card-advantage engine.

Wins by: Flooding the board with cheap creatures and tokens on turns 1-3. Adeline, Guide of Souls flyers and Gut skeletons swing big. Lightning Helix, Burst Lightning, Phlage and Broadside Bombardiers finish the last 5-8 points.

Key cards: Adeline, Resplendent Cathar; Skullclamp; Phlage, Titan of Fire's Fury; Skyclave Apparition; Elite Spellbinder; Showdown of the Skalds; Serra Paragon; Fury; Lightning Helix; Mother of Runes.

Synergies: Skullclamp on 1-toughness bodies (Adeline tokens, Blade Splicer, Cathar Commando, Mother) draws 2 for 1 mana. Gut and Bombardiers sac Clue, Treasure or Golem tokens. Cheap spells fill the yard for Phlage escape. Paragon recasts Splicer, Apparition or Inspector.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with at least two 1-2 drops and both colors reachable; fetches count as either color. Mulligan 1-landers unless Mox Diamond plus a 1-drop. Mulligan 5+ landers without Skullclamp or Showdown. A 7 with Adeline and a turn-3 land is a snap keep.

Early turns: Turn 1 Mother of Runes, Guide of Souls or Thraben Inspector; Mother first if the opponent has removal. Turn 2 Magda, Robber, Splitskin Doll or Skullclamp. Turn 3 Adeline over everything. Play Inspiring Vantage on turns 1-3. Hold Cathar Commando as flash. Save Helix and Burst for blockers or reach.

Fast mana: Mox Diamond only. Pitch a spare land on turn 1 if it enables a turn-2 Adeline or Apparition, or a 1-drop plus Skullclamp. Don't pitch your second land on a 2-land hand. Riverpyre Verge taps only with a Mountain; fetch Plateau first.

Against aggro: Mother and Guide early, Splicer's first-strike Golem as a wall. Fury evoked (exile a red card) or hardcast clears X/1s. Spend Helix and Phlage on creatures, and gain life. Skullclamp grinds out card advantage. Race only once you're ahead on board.

Against control: Lead with Elite Spellbinder on turn 3 to tax their sweeper or counter. Commit 2-3 threats, not your whole hand; hold Adeline or Cathar Commando to rebuild after a wipe. Showdown and Robber refill your hand. Point burn at the face late. Escape Phlage repeatedly as the closer.
