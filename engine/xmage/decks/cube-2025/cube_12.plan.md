Archetype: BG(r) ramp-combo: Channel into Emrakul or Ulamog, backed by a midrange shell of Sheoldred, Grist, Minsc & Boo and Titania, with Zuran Orb lifegain and land-sacrifice tricks.

Wins by: Channel paying life into Emrakul (extra turn, annihilator 6) or Ulamog (exile two permanents). Otherwise ramp into Sheoldred, Minsc & Boo or Grist, or make a pile of 5/3s with Titania plus Zuran Orb or Verdant Catacombs.

Key cards: Channel, Emrakul the Aeons Torn, Ulamog the Ceaseless Hunger, Demonic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor, Zuran Orb, Titania Protector of Argoth, Sheoldred the Apocalypse, Minsc & Boo, Grist the Hunger Tide, Fire Covenant, Snuff Out, Elvish Mystic, Utopia Sprawl.

Synergies: After Channel, sacrifice tapped lands to Zuran Orb for +2 life each to fund Emrakul. Titania returns Verdant Catacombs, and each Orb sacrifice makes a 5/3. Minsc can -2 a pumped Boo to draw cards. Tarmogoyf and Pyrogoyf grow from fetches, tutors and removal.

Mulligan: Keep Channel plus an Eldrazi or a tutor with a GG source. Otherwise keep 2-4 lands, a ramp piece (Mystic, Sprawl, Caryatid or Talisman) and a threat or removal spell. Mulligan hands that only have Eldrazi and no Channel or tutor, and 0-1 land hands without a dork.

Early turns: T1 Elvish Mystic or Utopia Sprawl on a Forest; Inquisition first against combo or control. T2 Channel to Emrakul if able, or Tarmogoyf or Caryatid. T3 Grist. T4 Sheoldred, Minsc & Boo or Titania. Keep the tutors until you can cast what you find next turn.

Fast mana: Use Mox Pearl to help a T1-2 Channel (each mana saves a life) or to cast Pentad Prism with a second color. Vampiric Tutor at end of turn for Channel if you hold an Eldrazi, or for Emrakul if you hold Channel. Demonic Tutor gets whichever piece is missing; without either, get Sheoldred.

Against aggro: Pay life carefully, since Channel needs 15 life for Emrakul or 10 for Ulamog. Use Fire Covenant and Snuff Out on their key creatures and wall with Caryatid. Sheoldred and Zuran Orb restore life. Grist -2 removes a threat. Prefer Ulamog if your life is low.

Against control: Emrakul can't be countered and is protected from colored spells, so Channel into it is the plan. Inquisition strips their cheap counters or removal first. Resolve Grist and Minsc early and pressure with Goyfs. Use Pest Infestation or Ulamog against artifact and enchantment locks, and Nexus late.
