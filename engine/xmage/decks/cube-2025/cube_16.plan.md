Archetype: Esper-splash-green Reanimator/Flash combo. Put a fatty (Archon of Cruelty, Atraxa, Torsten) into the graveyard via Flash or Bone Shards, then bring it back. Flash + Worldspine Wurm is a second, separate kill.
Wins by: Turn 2-4 Flash on Worldspine Wurm (decline to pay, it dies, you get three 5/5 tramplers), or a reanimated Archon of Cruelty / Atraxa taking over. Backup: Urza with Retrofitter Foundry, Quantum Riddler and Abhorrent Oculus beats.
Key cards: Flash, Worldspine Wurm, Archon of Cruelty, Atraxa, Necromancy, Persist, Unburial Rites, Life // Death, Bone Shards, Vampiric Tutor, Force of Will, Ephemerate.
Synergies: Flash on Archon or Torsten gets the ETB, then the creature dies into the yard for reanimation (Torsten also leaves seven Soldiers). Persist only works on Archon (Atraxa and Torsten are legendary). Ephemerate on a reanimated Archon or Atraxa repeats the ETB. Wurm can't be reanimated; it shuffles away.
Mulligan: Keep 7s with 2+ lands plus a combo half (Flash, Wurm, a reanimation spell or Vampiric Tutor), or a full combo. Mull hands with no lands, or with only fatties and no way to cheat them. Bone Shards + reanimation spell + fatty is a keep.
Early turns: T1 Mother of Runes or Retrofitter Foundry. T2 hold up Flash or Remand. Bone Shards (discard Archon/Atraxa) then Persist/Necromancy/Death. Thundertrap Trainer finds Flash or Necromancy. Stock Up digs. Discard Wurm only as a Bone Shards cost when it's otherwise dead.
Fast mana: None besides Urza tapping artifacts. Vampiric Tutor is the "one-shot": cast it at the opponent's end step for Flash (if Wurm or Archon is in hand) or Necromancy (if a fatty is in the yard). Don't fire it for value.
Against aggro: Mother, Dismember and Bone Shards buy time. Archon via Persist or Necromancy swings the race (sac, drain, gain 3). Flash-Wurm makes three blockers. Death costs life equal to MV, so with Death, reanimate Archon only above ~10 life. Prefer Persist or Necromancy.
Against control: Bait with Remand/Stock Up first. Go off when Force of Will is backup (pitch Quantum Riddler or Stock Up). Necromancy at instant speed at end step dodges sorcery-speed answers (it gets sacrificed at cleanup, so Archon's ETB is the value). Mother protects the reanimated threat.
