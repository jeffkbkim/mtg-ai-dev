Archetype: Mono-red aggro splashing white (Boros), with a low curve of hasty threats, burn and reach. Mox Emerald is only a colorless accelerant here.

Wins by: Dropping 1- and 2-drops on turns 1-2 and attacking every turn. Kari Zev, Rabblemaster and Copter carry the damage, then Chain Lightning, Lavamancer, Bombardment, Hazoret pings and Fiery Confluence (6 face damage) close the last 6-10 points.

Key cards: Goblin Rabblemaster, Smuggler's Copter, Ajani Nacatl Pariah, Kari Zev, Carnage Interpreter, Goblin Bombardment, Fury, Hazoret the Fervent, Fiery Confluence, Swords to Plowshares, Chain Lightning, Grim Lavamancer.

Synergies: Ajani flips when the Cat token dies (block, Bombardment sac), and his 0 then shoots. Inti, Seasoned Pyromancer, Copter loots and FOMO discards feed Ivora counters and Inti card exile. Probe, Manamorphose and Rage turn on DRC and Lavamancer. Interpreter empties your hand for Hazoret.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 landers that include a 1- or 2-drop threat. Mox Emerald counts as half a land. Ship hands with 5+ lands, and 1-landers without Mox plus two 1-drops. Plains-only hands with red cards are mulligans. Bo1: lean toward keeping any functional 7.

Early turns: Lead with DRC, Epicure or Lavamancer on T1, then Ajani, Kari, Inti or Copter on T2 and Rabblemaster or Interpreter on T3. Crew Copter with a 1-drop. Fetch Jetmir's Garden with Wooded Foothills when you need white. Hold Swords and Chain Lightning for blockers or utility creatures, and save Mine Collapse to sacrifice a spare Mountain.

Fast mana: Mox Emerald on T1 enables a 2-drop plus a 1-drop, or Rabblemaster on T2. Arena of Glory gives haste to Rabblemaster, Hazoret or Fury for surprise damage. Cast Manamorphose whenever it is free, for card flow and DRC triggers. Cycle Jetmir's Garden late.

Against aggro: Trade early and value Kari's first strike and Ajani's two bodies. Fiery Confluence in 1-damage modes wipes tokens and x/1s. Hard-cast Fury when possible, and evoke it only to stay alive. Swords their best threat, then race once stable, with Bombardment as a finisher.

Against control: Overextend carefully. Hold one threat back, plus Hazoret and Seasoned Pyromancer for after a sweeper. Bait counterspells with 2-drops before Rabblemaster. Use Probe to plan around their hand. Point burn at their face once they are at 8 or less, and keep the Hazoret activation going.
