Archetype: Grixis midrange-tempo. Cheap interaction, Mana Drain and fast artifacts into card-advantage threats (Fable, Dack, Karn, Seedshark). Power (Time Walk, Yawgmoth's Will, Demonic Tutor) pushes it ahead.
Wins by: Seedshark incubators, Fable's Goblin and Treasures, Barrowgoyf lifelink beats and a big Kappa Cannoneer. Karn constructs and Retrofitter thopters finish. Fiery Confluence (6 to face) and Chain Lightning close games.
Key cards: Sol Ring, Mana Drain, Fable of the Mirror-Breaker, Demonic Tutor, Time Walk, Yawgmoth's Will, Fury, Chrome Host Seedshark, Karn, Scion of Urza, Kappa Cannoneer, Dack Fayden.
Synergies: Snapcaster or Yawgmoth's Will rebuys Time Walk, Tutor, Chain or Hymn. Seedshark incubates off every noncreature spell. Artifacts (Star, Foundry, Talisman, Sol Ring, incubators, Treasures) improvise Kappa and grow Karn constructs. Fable chapter II and Dack +1 filter dead cards.
Mulligan: Keep 2-4 landers with a play by turn 2-3. Sol Ring plus any land is a keep. Mull no-landers and all-ritual hands. Ship 1-landers unless they hold Sol Ring or Talisman plus a cheap play. On 6, keep any functional land/spell mix.
Early turns: T1 fetch, Sol Ring, Star or Foundry. Hold Brainstorm until a fetch can shuffle. T2 Magda, Talisman, Hymn or hold Mana Drain/Abrade. T3 Fable or Seedshark. Hold Snapcaster for a flash rebuy of your best spent spell. Crack fetches for the color you lack.
Fast mana: Sol Ring always T1. Use Dark Ritual for a T1 Hymn, T2 Fable or Seedshark, or Yawgmoth's Will with a stocked yard. Don't Ritual into one small spell. Spend Mana Drain mana on Karn, Kappa or Foundry. Tutor for Time Walk early, Will late, Fury vs creatures.
Against aggro: Lead with Chain, Abrade and Fury (evoke by exiling a red card if behind). Use Confluence 1-damage modes vs go-wide. Barrowgoyf lifelink stabilizes. Skip Hymn, Brainstorm and Dack early. Tutor finds Fury or Confluence.
Against control: Hymn T2 (Ritual T1 if possible). Deploy threats at instant speed or into their tapped mana: Snapcaster, Fable, Dack. Hold Mana Drain for their key spell. Cannoneer's ward 4 is hard to answer. Pace threats one at a time. Will for a final burst.
