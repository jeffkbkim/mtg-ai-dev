Archetype: Naya (GW base, light red) aggro-midrange: cheap white creatures, mana dorks and fast mana, topped by Phlage, the two Overlords, Bloodbraid Elf and Showdown of the Skalds.

Wins by: Flooding the board with 1-2 drops on turns 1-3, then turning the corner with Overlord ETB/attack triggers, Phlage burn and escape, and Flickerwisp/Spellbinder fliers. Clean blockers with Swords, Oust and Phelia, and protect threats with Giver of Runes.

Key cards: Ocelot Pride, Guide of Souls, Ajani Nacatl Pariah, Phlage, Overlord of the Mistmoors, Overlord of the Boilerbilges, Showdown of the Skalds, Bloodbraid Elf, Swords to Plowshares, Mana Crypt. The deck has no tutors, so card flow comes from Showdown, Rumble, Inspector and Doll.

Synergies: Guide/Ocelot plus any lifegain (Phlage, Swords on your own creature, Guide triggers) makes Cats. Ajani flips when Cat tokens or Ocelot die. Phelia/Flickerwisp re-trigger an Overlord, Spellbinder or Inspector. Fetches add Springheart landfall. Fetching Temple Garden enables Utopia Sprawl.

Mulligan: Keep 2+ lands with 2+ one-drops, or any hand with Crypt or a dork plus a 3-4 drop. Ship 0-1 landers without Crypt, Pilgrim or Caryatid. Ship all-top-end hands with no early plays. Mox Jet counts only as colorless; don't keep it as a land substitute.

Early turns: Turn 1 Ocelot or Guide over Inspector; Pilgrim or Sprawl if holding a 3-4 drop. Turn 2 Ajani. Save Phelia (flash) for the opponent's end step. Crack fetches for Temple Garden or Sacred Foundry as colors demand. Shock untapped only when it enables a play.

Fast mana: Mana Crypt plus a land on turn 1 means Phelia/Ajani plus a one-drop, or turn 2 Showdown, Bloodbraid or impending Overlord. Mox Jet pays generic costs only, so dump it early for Ajani or Rumble. Drop Crypt early when ahead. Late, Crypt's flips hurt; cut it only if racing.

Against aggro: Mulligan toward cheap creatures. Swords and Oust their best early threat. Phlage on turn 3 kills a creature and gains 3. Guide/Ocelot lifegain swings races. Impending Boilerbilges for 4 damage each turn. Hold Giver of Runes for your key blocker. Don't take shock or fetch life loosely.

Against control: Deploy 2 threats at a time, never overextend into sweepers. Spellbinder taxes their sweeper or counterspell. Bloodbraid and Showdown give card advantage through counters. Save Reprieve to protect Overlord or Showdown. Giver of Runes blanks spot removal. Escape Phlage late for inevitability.
