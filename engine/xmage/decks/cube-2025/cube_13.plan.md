Archetype: Rakdos aggro-midrange splashing green (Grist, Questing Druid, Pest Infestation). Cheap red threats and efficient removal, with Mox Emerald and Mana Vault for fast starts and Bob/Preacher/Elegy for card advantage.
Wins by: Early 2-drops plus Smuggler's Copter chip in, burn and removal clear blockers, then Nova Hellkite, Laelia, Pyrogoyf or Draconautics Engineer's 4/4 dragon finish. Burst Lightning, Stomp and Ghostfire Slice close games to the face.
Key cards: Dark Confidant, Smuggler's Copter, Grist, the Hunger Tide, Laelia, the Blade Reforged, Bonecrusher Giant, Nova Hellkite, Go for the Throat, Snuff Out, Preacher of the Schism, Mana Vault.
Synergies: Copter loots with Ivora (discard = +1/+1 counter) and fills graveyard types for Pyrogoyf. Laelia and Seek the Beast exile cards for value. Magda's Treasures are only from herself. Karakas saves your Kari Zev, Laelia or Ivora from removal by returning them to hand.
Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands (Mox counts) with a 2-drop or removal. Mox Emerald plus a black/red land is strong. Ship 0-1 landers without Mox+Vault and 6-landers. Library of Alexandria alone is not a land you can count on; this deck rarely sits at 7 cards.
Early turns: Lead with Dark Confidant or Copter (crew with any 1-power), then Kari Zev, Ivora or Questing Druid. Fetch Forest with Misty early only if you hold green. Name Swamp with Multiversal Passage to enable Snuff Out. Play Bauble whenever it's free.
Fast mana: Mox Emerald turn 1 into a 2-drop or turn-2 Grist/Bonecrusher. Mana Vault turn 1 means turn-2 Nova Hellkite via warp or full cast, or Laelia plus a 2-drop. Don't untap Vault if paying 4 costs your turn; take the 1 damage. Avoid Vault plus Bob when low on life.
Against aggro: Prioritize removal: Burst, Stomp, Go for the Throat and free Snuff Out on tempo turns. Preacher (deathtouch 2/4) and Elegy (lifelink) stabilize. Grist +1 makes chump insects or -2 kills; Pest Infestation for X=1-2 makes blockers. Hold Bob back if life gets tight.
Against control: Deploy threats one or two at a time and keep pressure with Bob, Copter, Kari Zev and hasty Laelia/Nova. Dreams of Steel and Oil strips a key creature or artifact. Pest Infestation kills their mana rocks and enchantments. Save burn for the final points to the face.
