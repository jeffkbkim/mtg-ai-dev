Archetype: Boros aggro (RW burn-heavy creature curve). Fourteen one- and two-drops, eight burn spells to close, and Phlage/Nemesis/Hellkite as the top end.

Wins by: Flooding the board turns 1-3 with cheap hasty or evasive threats, using burn to clear blockers early, then pointing Bolt, Chain, Helix, Fireblast and Phlage at the face for the last 6-10 points.

Key cards: Phlage, Titan of Fire's Fury; Ocelot Pride; Guide of Souls; Screaming Nemesis; Kari Zev, Skyship Raider; Lightning Bolt; Lightning Helix; Fireblast; Bonecrusher Giant; Elite Spellbinder; Nova Hellkite.

Synergies: Guide of Souls plus creature drops and Helix/Phlage lifegain triggers Ocelot Pride's cat. Burn and cheap spells fill the graveyard for Phlage escape and Pyrogoyf. Arena of Glory or Draconautics Engineer give haste to Nemesis and Bonecrusher. Death-Greeter's Champion backup gives Kari double strike.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with at least two one- or two-drops, and ideally both colors (Plateau, Verge, Canyon). Mulligan 1-landers, 5+ landers, and hands of only burn with no creatures. A 7 with one W one-drop, one R two-drop and Bolt is ideal.

Early turns: Turn 1, play Ocelot Pride, Guide of Souls or Lightstall Inquisitor (Giver only if no other one-drop). Turn 2, Kari Zev, Robber of the Rich or Generous Plunderer, or two one-drops. Turn 3, Elite Spellbinder to strip their answer, or Nemesis. Bolt blockers early; save the last burn for face.

Fast mana: There is no artifact fast mana. Arena of Glory exert gives haste to Nemesis or Bonecrusher on turn 3-4, or reaches 5 for a hasty Hellkite. Warp Nova Hellkite on turn 3 for a hit plus a ping. Fireblast costs two Mountains (Plateau counts), so cast it only for lethal or a must-kill. Crack Sunbaked Canyon when flooding.

Against aggro: Keep Giver of Runes and Kari Zev as blockers, and kill their one-drops on sight with Bolt, Chain or Stomp. Fiery Confluence as 3x1 to each creature sweeps tokens. Helix and Phlage swing the race; hold Phlage escape to stabilize. Don't overextend your X/1s into their pingers.

Against control: Lead with Lightstall Inquisitor and Elite Spellbinder to tax their sweeper or counter. Deploy two threats at a time, not four; keep Nemesis, Hellkite and Phlage escape in reserve. Robber of the Rich and Plunderer pressure their hand and mana. Hold instant burn plus Fireblast for an end-of-turn burn-out.
