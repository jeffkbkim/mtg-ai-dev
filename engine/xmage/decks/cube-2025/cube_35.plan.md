Archetype: Mardu (W/B/R) removal-heavy midrange with a light blue splash for Psychic Frog, Fallen Shinobi and Treasure Cruise. You kill everything early, then win with card-advantage threats.

Wins by: Answering their first threats with cheap removal, then closing with Carnage Interpreter, Elegy Acolyte, The Wandering Emperor, Gut's 4/1 Skeletons and Restless Fortress drains. Burn (Lightning Helix, Legion Extruder) finishes the last points.

Key cards: Carnage Interpreter, The Wandering Emperor, Elegy Acolyte, Seasoned Pyromancer, Fire Covenant, Vindicate, Damn, Sunfall, Psychic Frog, Galvanic Discharge, Treasure Cruise.

Synergies: Carnage Interpreter's Clues feed Legion Extruder Golems and Gut sacrifices. Jadar's decayed Zombies are free Gut fodder. Fetches and cheap spells fill the graveyard for Unholy Heat delirium and Treasure Cruise delve. Unearth returns Pyromancer, Interpreter, Frog or Jadar.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with at least two colors and one cheap removal spell or 2-drop. Mull 7-card hands with 0-1 or 6+ lands. Don't keep hands that need blue early. Frog, Shinobi and Cruise are bonuses, not keep reasons.

Early turns: Fetch flexibly, prioritizing B and R first, then Tundra or Triome for W/U. T1 Inquisition, or hold Galvanic Discharge/Unholy Heat. T2 Jadar, Frog or Generous Plunderer. T3 Carnage Interpreter, ideally after emptying your hand. Play tapped lands (Triome, Fortress) on turns with spare mana.

Fast mana: The deck has none. Generous Plunderer Treasures and Carnage Interpreter Clues are your ramp. Spend Treasure to jump to The Wandering Emperor or Sunfall a turn early, or to double-spell removal plus threat. Only crack Clues when you have spare mana.

Against aggro: Mull for cheap removal. Trade one-for-one with Discharge, Heat, Go for the Throat and Helix. Pyrokinesis and Fire Covenant are blowouts against swarms. Hold Damn's overload or Sunfall for wide boards. Elegy Acolyte's lifelink stabilizes. Fetch and shock carefully.

Against control: Lead with Inquisition and cheap threats like Jadar (recurring value) and Frog, then Pyromancer. Don't overcommit into sweepers. Flash in The Wandering Emperor at their end step. Vindicate their planeswalkers or engines. Restless Fortress dodges sorcery removal. Reload with Treasure Cruise and Interpreter's Clues.
