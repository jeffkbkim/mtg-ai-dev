Archetype: Jeskai artifact-tempo walkers with light splashes. Fast mana drops Teferi, Time Raveler, Dack Fayden or Minsc & Boo early, then cheap removal and soft counters protect them. Green for Minsc and black for Psychic Frog are splashes.

Wins by: Planeswalker pressure (Minsc & Boo's Boo pump and fling, Teferi, Dack) plus hasty threats: Broadside Bombardiers, Cori-Steel Cutter Monks, a big Nettlecyst germ. Lightning Bolt, Lightning Helix, Legion Extruder and Bombardiers boasts finish the opponent.

Key cards: Mana Crypt, Mana Vault, Teferi, Time Raveler, Minsc & Boo, Timeless Heroes, Dack Fayden, Balance, Solitude, Broadside Bombardiers, Nettlecyst, Cori-Steel Cutter, Psychic Frog.

Synergies: Artifacts (Crypt, Vault, Talismans, Converter, Extruder, Cutter, Nettlecyst) turn on Mox Opal, pump Nettlecyst, and fuel Extruder Golems and Bombardiers boasts. A Bombardiers boast saccing spent Mana Vault deals 3. Solitude evokes by pitching Oust, March, Reprieve, Helix or No More Lies. Frog can discard excess lands.

Mulligan: Keep 2-3 lands plus Crypt, Vault or a Talisman with a 3-4 drop walker. Mulligan 7s with no white or red source, or all spells. Zero-land Crypt hands are a mulligan. Don't count on the Taiga/Confluence/Unity splash for Minsc in a keep. Mox Opal alone is not mana.

Early turns: Fetch for Tundra or Taiga first. T1 Crypt into Talisman, Extruder or Cutter. T2-3: Teferi if they have instants, otherwise Minsc or Dack. Open with Bolt or Extruder on their mana creatures. Hold Miscalculation, No More Lies and Reprieve when you're ahead.

Fast mana: Crypt always comes down on turn 1. Spend Vault on a turn-1/2 walker, Nettlecyst plus Cutter, or Solitude hard-cast. Don't Vault for small stuff, since it costs you 4 to untap. Opal comes down once you have 3 artifacts. Before Balance, dump artifacts and walkers so Balance hits only lands, hands and creatures.

Against aggro: Kill early drops with Bolt, Helix, Oust and Extruder. Flash Solitude is a blowout; evoke it if behind. When their board and hand are bigger, Balance while your walkers or artifacts stay. Minsc's Boo blocks and flings. Skip Converter loot and don't Vault yourself into damage; Helix can stabilize.

Against control: Lead with Teferi to stop their instant-speed counters and flash plays. Drop threats one per turn: Bombardiers (haste), Frog, Minsc. Hold No More Lies, Miscalculation and Reprieve for their wraths or bombs. Dack steals artifacts. Converter and Booster Tutor give card flow; burn their face late.
