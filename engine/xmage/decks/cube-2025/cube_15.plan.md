Archetype: White Weenie aggro with a light red splash (Comet, Death-Greeter's Champion) and a blue splash for Fractured Identity. Low curve, protection creatures and planeswalkers.

Wins by: Flooding the board with 1-2 drops, then pumping and protecting them. Use Intrepid Adversary, Luminarch Aspirant, Virtue of Loyalty, Sanguine Evangelist battle cry and the Gideon emblem. Planeswalkers and removal clear the way while Mother and Giver keep key threats alive.

Key cards: Ocelot Pride, Mother of Runes, Giver of Runes, Luminarch Aspirant, Intrepid Adversary, Elite Spellbinder, The Wandering Emperor, Gideon Ally of Zendikar, Parallax Wave, Virtue of Loyalty, Path to Exile, March of Otherworldly Light, Black Lotus.

Synergies: Ocelot Pride with lifelink from Adversary, Emperor's -2 or Serra Paragon. Parallax Wave with Containment Priest exiles creatures permanently. Wave can also blink your own Blade Splicer, Spellbinder or Loran. Glimmer Lens draws when going wide. Paragon recasts Splicer or Aspirant. Comet's 3 rebuys 2-drops.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with at least two 1-2 drops, or Lotus plus a land and plays. Mother, Giver, Pride or Aspirant on turn 1-2 makes a strong keep. Mulligan 0-1 landers without Lotus, and 5+ landers. Splash cards in hand don't justify a keep without a red or blue source.

Early turns: Lead with Ocelot Pride or Mother of Runes on turn 1, then Aspirant or Adversary on turn 2. Play Inspiring Vantage or Seachrome Coast within your first three lands so they enter untapped. Crack Arid Mesa for Tundra or Plains early, or for a Mountain if you hold Comet or Champion. Hold Mana Tithe for opposing turn 1-3 plays.

Fast mana: Lotus on turn 1 for Plains, Mother plus Aspirant, or a turn-1 three-drop like Spellbinder or Blade Splicer. Best uses are a turn-2 Gideon, Emperor or Parallax Wave, or Adversary with valor paid. Don't fire Lotus for a single 1-drop; save it to burst out a walker.

Against aggro: Mother and Giver protect blockers. Golems with first strike and Ocelot Pride hold the ground. Path, March and Emperor's -2 handle threats, and Wave holds off attackers for several turns. Lifelink from Adversary and Pride races well. Flash in Emperor or Phelia during their combat.

Against control: Spellbinder their best counterspell or wrath. Use Mana Tithe early and deploy only 2-3 threats at a time to play around sweepers. Gideon and Virtue (cast as the Ardenvale Fealty adventure first) rebuild after a wipe. Loran answers artifacts and enchantments, and Fractured Identity steals their bomb. Protect your attackers with Mother and Giver.
