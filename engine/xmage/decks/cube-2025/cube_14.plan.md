Archetype: Dimir artifact tempo-control splashing white (Esper-ish), with Grim Monolith/Talismans/Academy ramp into Myr Battlesphere, Coveted Jewel and Displacer Kitten value. Pyrite Spellbomb has no red source, so treat it as a 1-mana artifact that cycles for {1}.

Wins by: Psychic Frog and Baleful Strix chip early, then Myr Battlesphere, Saga Constructs, Celestial Colonnade or a copied threat close. Hullbreacher and Cryptic Command keep the opponent off balance. Tezzeret's -7 emblem is a backup finisher.

Key cards: Urza's Saga, Grim Monolith, Psychic Frog, Displacer Kitten, Tezzeret Cruel Captain, Myr Battlesphere, Cryptic Command, Subtlety, Swords to Plowshares, Coveted Jewel, Thieving Skydiver, Tishana's Tidebinder.

Synergies: Kitten flickers Monolith (untaps it), Jewel (draws 3, untaps) or Tezzeret (resets loyalty) on each noncreature spell. Saga III or Tezzeret -3 fetch Mox, Spellbomb or Map. Map finds Academy or Saga. Image/Metamorph copy Battlesphere, Jewel or Kitten. Frog eats extra lands.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with a 2-drop (Frog, Strix, Skydiver) or a mana rock. Mox plus Monolith hands with 2 lands are great. Mull 7s that are all 4+ drops with no acceleration, and 1-landers unless they hold Mox plus Monolith. You need blue: Island sources matter most.

Early turns: Play Saga early if you have colorless mana; make Constructs on chapter II, and on III grab Chrome Mox (or Map if you need Academy). Lead with Frog or Strix and hold flash creatures (Hullbreacher, Tidebinder, Subtlety) up on the opponent's turn. Use Strand/shocks smartly: Watery Grave is your best UB land.

Fast mana: Chrome Mox: imprint a redundant card (Booster Tutor, extra copy effect), never Cryptic or Kitten. Turn 1 Monolith powers out Tezzeret or Kitten on turn 2, or Jewel on turn 3. Monolith plus Jewel taps for 6 again. Academy only with 2+ artifacts. Grab artifacts with Skydiver kicker.

Against aggro: Swords the first real threat. Strix and Frog block well, and Tidebinder stops ETB or equip tricks. Pay life on shocks sparingly. Don't land Coveted Jewel versus open attackers unless you can protect it. Cryptic tapping their team buys a turn for Battlesphere or Colonnade.

Against control: Hold Subtlety, Tidebinder and Cryptic for their bombs and walkers. Hullbreacher punishes draw spells. Grind with Strix, Thirst, Cruise and Takenuma. Make Colonnade and Saga Constructs as hard-to-counter threats. Skydiver steals their rocks. Bait counters with Jewel before Kitten.
