Archetype: Four-color Bant control splashing black. The core is white-blue counters and planeswalkers. Green adds Nadu, Up the Beanstalk and Omnath. Black adds Atraxa, Crabomination and Pillage the Bog.

Wins by: Counter or remove their threats, then lock the board with Teferi, Hero of Dominaria or Gideon. Finish with Atraxa, a Celestial Colonnade beater, Gideon knights, or Omnath landfall damage. Shelldock Isle can cast a free bomb once a library is at 20 cards or fewer.

Key cards: Force of Will, Force of Negation, Counterspell, Solitude, Wrath of God, Teferi Time Raveler, Teferi Hero of Dominaria, Nadu, Up the Beanstalk, Leyline Binding, Atraxa. Pillage the Bog is your tutor: dig for Wrath of God against aggro and Teferi Hero against control.

Synergies: Equipping Shadowspear onto Nadu or another flyer triggers Nadu, and so does Ephemerate on a flyer. Phantasmal Image copying Nadu doubles the triggers. Up the Beanstalk draws off any 5+ spell, including an alt-cost Force of Will, Solitude or Leyline Binding. Duals and Ketria Triome give domain, so Leyline Binding usually costs W. Ephemerate replays Solitude.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 lands with at least one W/U source plus a counter or removal spell. Ship hands of all expensive cards. Ship hands with no blue or white source. A hand with Force of Will plus a blue card to pitch, plus lands, is a snap keep.

Early turns: Fetch with Arid Mesa early to thin and enable Omnath later. Play tapped lands (Archive, Triome, Colonnade, Shelldock) on turns you won't need mana. T2: Up the Beanstalk or Thieving Skydiver; hold Counterspell or No More Lies when the opponent is likely to cast something key. T3: Nadu or Teferi, Time Raveler.

Fast mana: Grim Monolith only makes colorless mana. Cast it T2 to jump to Teferi, Hero of Dominaria or Gideon on T3, or to pay Crabomination's emerge by sacrificing it (costs {3}{B}{B}). Don't drop it into a board where they can bounce or destroy artifacts. A kicked Skydiver for X=1 can steal their artifact.

Against aggro: Mulligan toward cheap interaction. Evoke Solitude freely (pitch Ephemerate or Gideon), and save Wrath of God for 2+ creatures. Shadowspear on a blocker gains life. Omnath plus a land gains 4. Stabilize with Gideon's knights, then Atraxa.

Against control: Keep Force of Will and Force of Negation for their bombs and planeswalkers. Land threats at instant speed (Subtlety, Colonnade) or on turns they tap out. Teferi, Time Raveler stops their counters, so resolve walkers right after it. Shelldock Isle and Up the Beanstalk card flow win long games.
