Archetype: Mono-red aggro (R+B in name only; Carnage Interpreter is cast off red hybrid) with fast mana, cheap threats, burn and a top end of Chandra, Nova Hellkite and Overlord of the Boilerbilges.

Wins by: Getting cheap threats down early, adding Goblin Rabblemaster, Smuggler's Copter or Laelia, then finishing with Burst Lightning, Ghostfire Slice, Chandra +1, Overlord or Pyrogoyf triggers to the face.

Key cards: Mana Crypt, Ancient Tomb, Goblin Rabblemaster, Smuggler's Copter, Fable of the Mirror-Breaker, Carnage Interpreter, Laelia, the Blade Reforged, Chandra, Torch of Defiance, Overlord of the Boilerbilges, Shadowspear.

Synergies: Copter, Fable, Fear of Missing Out and Tersa loot to pump Ivora and fill the graveyard for Dragon's Rage Channeler, Pyrogoyf and FOMO delirium. Death-Greeter's Champion backup gives Rabblemaster or Laelia double strike. Draconautics Engineer gives haste to fresh threats.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 mana sources with at least one Mountain and one or two plays costing 1-2. Mana Crypt or Ancient Tomb plus a 3-drop is a snap keep. Mull one-landers without fast mana, and hands of only 4-6 drops. Mox Diamond counts as a land only if you have a spare land to discard.

Early turns: Lead with Dragon's Rage Channeler, Hired Claw or Kellan, then Copter or Ivora on turn 2. With fast mana, slam Rabblemaster, Fable or Laelia on turn 1-2. Hold Flame Slash and Burst Lightning for blockers. Play Arena of Glory after a Mountain so it enters untapped.

Fast mana: Use Mana Crypt and Ancient Tomb immediately for a turn-1 3-drop: Rabblemaster, Laelia, Fable or Carnage Interpreter (empty your hand first). Pitch only an excess land to Mox Diamond. Exert Arena of Glory for a hasty Nova Hellkite or Laelia. Use Library of Alexandria only on slow opening hands.

Against aggro: Trade early. Flame Slash, Burst Lightning, Ghostfire Slice and Chandra -3 kill their best creature. FOMO and Hired Claw block well, and Nova Hellkite pings an X/1. Shadowspear on a flyer races with lifelink. Watch Ancient Tomb and Mana Crypt damage. Overlord impending acts as removal.

Against control: Pressure fast with several threats rather than one big one. Haste threats (Laelia, Tersa, Nova Hellkite, dashed Death-Greeter's Champion) dodge sorcery-speed removal. Save burn for reach to the face. Name their key artifact or planeswalker with Phyrexian Revoker. Chandra and Fable give card advantage. Shadowspear beats hexproof.
