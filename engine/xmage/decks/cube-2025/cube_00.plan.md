Archetype: Boros aggro (RW) with a low curve of 1- and 2-drops, Skullclamp card flow and burn-style removal. Red is splashed lightly over a white core, with Phlage and Comet as the gold top end.

Wins by: Fast pressure from 1-2 drops, then reach to finish. Reach comes from menace/first strike (Kari Zev, Plunderer), Gut's 4/1 Skeletons, Nova Hellkite hasting in, Phlage burn (3 per cast, then escape for repeat 3-damage attacks) and Bonecrusher Stomp to the face.

Key cards: Skullclamp, Ocelot Pride, Guide of Souls, Thalia, Voice of Victory, Phelia, Elite Spellbinder, Phlage, Solitude, Bonecrusher Giant, Gut, Nova Hellkite, Comet. Ancient Tomb and Mox Ruby speed out the 2-3 drops.

Synergies: Skullclamp on X/1s (Thalia, Splitskin Doll, Cat and Warrior tokens) draws 2 per death. Guide and Ocelot gain life from creature entries, so Ocelot makes Cats. Voice's Warriors feed Gut and Inti. Inti's discard finds cards and fills the graveyard for Phlage escape. Witch Enchanter can be played as a land.

Mulligan: Keep 2-4 mana sources (Moxen count) with at least one 1-2 drop and a white source. Skullclamp plus any creature is a keep. Mull hands with no white mana, 5+ lands and no Clamp, or all 3-5 drops. Seven-card 1-landers are only fine with Mox plus Tomb.

Early turns: T1 Ocelot Pride or Guide of Souls, otherwise Thraben Inspector. Play Thalia early against noncreature decks. T2 Kari Zev, Plunderer, Phelia or Voice. Clamp once you have X/1s or tokens. Save Flame Slash and Portable Hole for blockers or mana dorks. Keep Phelia as a flash ambush or blocker.

Fast mana: Mox Ruby/Jet on T1 lets you play a 1-drop plus Clamp, or a T1 two-drop. Mox Jet only makes generic mana here. Ancient Tomb casts Spellbinder, Bonecrusher or Gut on T2; mind the life loss against aggro. Play Plains/Mountain early so Vantage and Verge come in untapped. Keep 3 real Mountains for Mine Collapse.

Against aggro: Trade aggressively and use removal on their best threats. Guide, Ocelot lifelink, Sage and Phlage are your lifegain. Bonecrusher is a 2-for-1. Solitude evoke (pitch a white card) answers a fast threat. Limit pain from Ancient Tomb, Confluence and Canyon. Kari Zev blocks well. Clamp grinds them out.

Against control: Go wide fast and hold some threats back to rebuild after a sweeper. Thalia taxes their spells and Voice stops counters and instant removal on your turn, so cast your big spells behind Voice. Elite Spellbinder strips their sweeper or bomb. Phlage escapes late, Inti and Clamp refuel, and Nova Hellkite dodges sorcery-speed answers. Hole their mana rocks.
