# Review of the 70 strong 17lands DSK decks

Made by `python3 play/review_decks.py` (claude-opus-5-5, effort medium, one headless review per deck with no tools; total cost $1.39, replies cached in `review_cache.json`). The reviewer saw the decklist with card text, the curve and counts, but not the record, rank or drafter, so its rating is of the build alone.

- Curve: nonland cards at mana value 1/2/3/4/5/6+ (a Room at its cheaper door, X spells at X=1). Removal: a rough rules-text tag, hard (soft in brackets: bounce, tap-down, can't-block auras).
- Archetype WR: 17lands' win rate for the deck's main colors, all DSK PremierDraft games, splashes combined.
- Red flags: 3+ main colors with fewer than 3 fixing lands; fewer than 5 two-drops or 5+ cards at 6+; lands outside 16-18; archetype WR below 53%.
- Tier 2 (★★, 14 decks): rating >= 4, no red flag, drafter win-rate bucket >= 0.60 (any rank). Every other deck is tier 1 (★). Ratings: 2: 13, 3: 42, 4: 15. Only 14 decks reach the rating with no red flag, and a strong-drafter gate (bucket >= 0.64 and rank diamond/mythic) would leave 2, so the drafter gate is the pool's own floor (every deck is a 7-win trophy by a drafter at bucket >= 0.60).
- Drop candidates (rating <= 2, 13 decks) are listed but kept: good_00 (weak archetype WUG 51.2%; colors, few creatures), good_03 (threat density, top end), good_05 (weak archetype URG 52.4%; colors, mana base), good_11 (few creatures, threat quality), good_13 (mana, colors), good_25 (few creatures, threats), good_31 (weak archetype WUG 51.2%; colors, mana), good_48 (few creatures, win conditions), good_49 (3 main colors, 2 fixing lands; weak archetype WUR 50.4%; colors, few creatures), good_51 (colors, mana), good_55 (3 main colors, 1 fixing lands; weak archetype WRG 47.9%; colors, mana), good_60 (few creatures, aura overload), good_61 (few creatures, threat quality).

- 17lands archetype WR (main colors, all players): WU 56.4% (30 decks), WR 57.0% (19 decks), UG 55.6% (11 decks), WUG 51.2% (2 decks), URG 52.4% (2 decks), RG 55.4% (2 decks), WUB 48.7% (1 decks), WUR 50.4% (1 decks), UBG 53.0% (1 decks), WRG 47.9% (1 decks); all decks 54.6%. Weak archetype: below 53%.
- Top-player GIH WR (drafter bucket >= 0.62) vs all-player GIH WR: Pearson 0.938 over 236 nonland cards; deck score from it vs deck score: Spearman 0.631 over 70 decks.
- 17lands' published card ratings could not be used: the endpoint returned 281 cards but only 362 games in all (0 cards with 500+ GIH games), whatever date range or user_group was asked.
