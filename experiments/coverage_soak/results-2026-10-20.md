# Coverage soak results, 2026-10-20

Run on the Mac mini: 600 runs × 4 games, seed 1, 4 parallel JVMs, commit `fddbf19`, XMage 1.4.62.
Our seat played biased-random moves; the other seat was XMage's AI. Raw output (decks, JVM logs, decisions,
`report.md`, `cards.json`) is in `~/mtg-data/soak/full-2026-10-20` on the mini, not in Git.

**Coverage:** 2,318 games finished. Every DSK card except the five basics was in at least one deck, and every one of
them reached the battlefield or the stack at least once. Reaching play once doesn't mean every line of rules text was
exercised.

## Real problems

1. **Hidden-information leak on "discard a card" costs paid by the opponent.** When an effect lets the opponent
   pay a discard cost (Osseous Sticktwister's delirium trigger 26 times, Sheltered by Ghosts once,
   Attack-in-the-Box's granted ability once), our seat was asked to pick a card, and the options were the names of
   the *other* player's hand. Seen in 13 runs, and the discarded card came out of the *opponent's* hand, so the game itself went wrong too.
   **Root cause (fixed 2026-10-20):** `PySeat.chooseTargets` listed the options with the *source's controller* as
   the choosing player, so an opponent's ability made our seat choose from the opponent's hand. XMage's own
   `HumanPlayer` uses `target.getAffectedAbilityControllerId(playerId)`, and the seat now does too.
   `engine/xmage/SeatChoiceCheck.java` reproduces it (FAIL before, PASS after). A 160-game soak after the fix
   showed no hand leaks.
2. **XMage's AI crashes on some blocks:** `IllegalArgumentException: AI can't find good blocker combination` ended
   3 runs (277, 349, 431). The stack trace names no card, so the decks in those runs are the suspects.
   **Root cause (2026-10-21):** in all three, our seat attacked with menace creatures (Irreverent Gremlin, or
   Gremlin tokens given menace by Midnight Mayhem), and XMage's AI couldn't find a legal block in 20 tries. That
   AI weakness is a known TODO in XMage's `Combat.selectBlockers`. A real XMage server then logs a warning and
   skips the block; it only throws because our runners put seats in test mode, where XMage fast-fails. SeatSpike and
   TwoSeatMatch now call `setFastFailInTestMode(false)`, so the AI just doesn't block that combat. Not reproduced on
   demand: replaying the three deck pairs for 90 games and 30 menace-heavy games gave no crash, so the trigger is rare.
   **Correction (2026-10-21, later):** building the boards directly (`engine/xmage/BlockCheck.java`) showed the AI
   blocks menace attackers legally on all the crash boards tried, so menace alone isn't the trigger. Chasing it found
   a different bug: XMage's AI plays out candidate moves on copies of the game, and the copies held a copy of our
   seat that still talked to Python. Our seat was answering questions about boards that never happened (run 349's
   last question before the crash was one of these: a Screaming Nemesis damage trigger during the AI's
   declare-blockers lookahead). The seat now answers in Java whenever `game.isSimulation()`, and counts it
   (`SIMULATED`). `engine/xmage/SimulationCheck.java` reproduces it deterministically (FAIL before, PASS after).
   On the 160-game soak (seed 11), 6 such questions reached Python before the fix and 0 after (5 answered in Java).
   That these simulated answers caused the blocker crash is likely, not proven; the crash itself still has no
   deterministic repro. Recorded decisions from before this fix can include a few of these make-believe states, but only in games
   against XMage's AI (soaks, spikes). Claude-vs-Claude games are unaffected: both seats are PySeat, which extends
   the base ComputerPlayer, and that never simulates.
3. **Moves the seat offers but XMage refuses** (`REJECTED`, 257 runs). Mostly casts or activations of Unidentified
   Hovership (669), Hedge Shredder (568), Seized from Slumber (400), Valgavoth's Onslaught (171) and Betrayer's
   Bargain (111). The random seat picks them, and the engine then refuses them. Probably cost or crew legality that
   the option list doesn't check. Harmless for random play, but a trained policy would waste probability on them.
   **Update 2026-10-21:** mostly Crew still offered after every other creature was tapped, so it was refused at
   every step. The seat now hides a move that was refused without asking it anything until the board, hand, life,
   graveyard, stack or mana pool changes. Refusals that follow the seat's own choices (a target, an alternative cost)
   are still only dropped for that priority window, since another choice could work. Same 160-game soak (seed 11):
   233 refusals before, 125 after, 64 of those after the seat's own choices.
   **Fixed 2026-10-22:** the leftover Crew refusals are an XMage bug: `CrewCost.canPay` checks "another untapped
   creature" without the vehicle as source, so a crewed, untapped vehicle counts toward its own Crew. The seat now
   offers Crew only when other untapped creatures have the power. Same soak: 34-45 Crew refusals before, 0 after.
4. **Abilities with no stable key:** Shard Token's "{2}, sacrifice: scry 1, draw" (70 runs), Marvin, Murderous
   Mimic's activated ability (46), and a few others. Also an unmapped alternative-cost choice,
   "Cast with alternative cost: {W}{U}{B}{R}{G}" (22 runs). These need entries in `AbilityKeys`.
   **Fixed 2026-10-22:** `AbilityKeys.key` now falls back to (a) the printed ability of another permanent the
   controller has, for Marvin's borrowed abilities (they get the lending card's key, e.g. "Tyvar, the Pummeler#0"),
   and (b) the granting card's "#g" abilities matched by class and rules text, for Niko's Shard tokens.
   `engine/xmage/KeyCheck.java` fails before, passes after. The alternative-cost choice is still unmapped.

## Not real

- **Leak checker false positives:** it matches card names as substrings, so "Murder" fires on Marvin, Murderous
  Mimic, "Saw" on Sawblade Skinripper, and "Marina Vendrell" on Marina Vendrell's Grimoire. That accounts for
  about 1,300 of the leak hits. Fixed 2026-10-21 (and 2026-10-22 for Gson's escaped apostrophe, "Marina Vendrell\u0027s"): `Observer.scanText` now matches whole names; planted leaks (`LEAKTEST=1`) are still caught, and the same 160-game soak went from 2 runs with leak reports to 0.
- **19 hangs and the database exceptions** came from the soak itself: parallel JVMs opening XMage's card database
  at the same moment ("Lock file recently modified"), so those JVMs never connected. `soak.py` now boots one JVM at
  a time and ignores those lines.
